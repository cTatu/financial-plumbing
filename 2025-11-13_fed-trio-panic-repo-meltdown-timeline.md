# Fed's Top Trio PANIC: Repo Market Meltdown Incoming? - Shocking Timeline Shows Fed Insiders' Concern

## 1. Thesis

Repo stress no longer looks like quarter-end noise: SOFR sits 2bp below the upper bound on a plain mid-November date with spreads as wide as Q3 end, while Powell plus former and current SOMA managers Logan and Perli all break the usual silence on SOFR and reserves within one month. The through-line is the $1.8 trillion 100-to-1 levered Cayman basis trade funding about 40% of Treasury purchases — the same structure that partly unwound on 2020 repo strain and now runs even larger — so wider SOFR-RRP spreads, sliding bank reserves, a $3.9 billion SRF tap, and an overshot TGA rebuild point to scarce reserves forcing balance-sheet expansion before a forced $1.8 trillion Treasury sale.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] Mid-November spreads matching quarter-end means stress reflects scarce reserves and willingness to lend rather than calendar window-dressing.
- [TAKE] Powell commenting on SOFR is itself the signal because the Fed chair normally avoids wonky repo-spread plumbing.
- [TAKE] The basis trade turns repo funding into solvency risk because 50-to-100x leverage means a small SOFR-leg move forces unwind of long cash Treasuries.
- [TAKE] A $1.8 trillion forced unwind would mean $1.8 trillion of cash-Treasury selling because basis shorts must cover by dumping the long leg.
- [TAKE] Normal money-market calm with stocks near highs plus worried insiders is more alarming than visible panic because the people with balance-sheet data see the constraint first.
