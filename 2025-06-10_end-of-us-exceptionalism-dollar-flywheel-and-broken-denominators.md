# End of US Exceptionalism? - The Dollar Flywheel and Broken Denominators

## 1. Thesis

America's $100-billion-per-month current-account deficit mechanically forces a matching capital-account surplus into dollar assets, and that flywheel plus a structurally strong dollar turned the S&P 500's 1,500% run since 1990 into 1,700-2,255% in yen, euro, and pound terms — but with $9-$10 trillion of that foreign equity exposure unhedged, 2025 dollar weakness already leaves foreigners down 5-6% while Americans print up 1-3%, previewing how repatriation and a deliberate reindustrialization devaluation would unwind US exceptionalism into nominal sideways gains that lose in gold, Bitcoin, and foreign-currency terms.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] The current-account deficit and capital-account surplus are the same transaction, so $100 billion per month must bid US stocks, Treasuries, and real estate.
- [TAKE] Dollar strength flatters foreign returns on the way in and punishes them on the way out because selling dollar assets means selling dollars for home currency.
- [TAKE] Unhedged exposure persists because hedging costs return and neither the dollar nor the S&P has required it for 35 years.
- [TAKE] Index dominance is a denominator illusion since Argentina up 51,000% in pesos and Turkey vertical in lira both collapse to ~100% or less once priced in dollars, gold, or Bitcoin.
- [TAKE] Reindustrialization requires a substantially weaker dollar from grossly overvalued levels, so administration success will read as DXY and gold weakness for dollar-only holders.
