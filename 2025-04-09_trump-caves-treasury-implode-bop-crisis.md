# Trump Caves as Treasury Bond Market Starts to Implode - An Emerging BOP Crisis?

## 1. Thesis

The bond market forced Trump's hand: after three days in which long-end yields surged into a tariff-driven growth shock, he paused tariffs for every country except China while hiking China duties further. The price action — stocks, bonds, and the dollar falling together, MOVE printing 171, and haven demand choosing yen, franc, and gold — meets every textbook balance-of-payments crisis checklist the video lays out. The deeper argument is that tariffs alone cannot close the gap because China answers with yuan devaluation and state-bank dollar selling, so only a Mar-a-Lago-style monetary reordering that weakens the dollar against the yuan and devalues all fiat against gold and scarce assets resolves the twin-deficit flywheel.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] [17:28] The three-day 51-basis-point surge reflects a mix of basis-trade unwind, fiscal fear, and foreign selling rather than any single identifiable cause.
- [TAKE] [32:55] Joint weakness in equities, long bonds, and the dollar meets the balance-of-payments crisis definition and voids the exorbitant-privilege defense.
- [TAKE] [25:58] Real yields rising 35 basis points while breakevens fall 6 basis points proves the selloff prices duration and deficit risk rather than inflation.
