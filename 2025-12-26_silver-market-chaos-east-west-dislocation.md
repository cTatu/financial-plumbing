# Breaking: Silver Market Chaos - Huge Dislocations Between East & West

## 1. Thesis

Silver's rip through $75 — up 6.3% on the day in a ~$258 billion single-session move, +112% in the second half — is the small-base, high-beta leg of the debasement trade: with gold at $31 trillion versus silver at $4.3 trillion, equal dollar flows move silver roughly seven times as far. The market is forward-pricing the negative-real-rate regime Washington's run-hot policy makes inevitable, which is why gold's +72% trailing year coincides with a positive 1.4% real 10-year yield and breaks a 50-to-60-year correlation. Underneath, the plumbing strains: a deeply negative 1-year swap spread, buyers standing for physical delivery despite vault and haulage costs, a 60%+ premium on China's only pure silver fund, and 531-ton Shanghai inventories at decade-plus lows — an Eastern physical bid the Western paper market no longer reflects.
