# Breaking: Emergency Fed Backstop Gets Hit - Repo Market Cracks Widen

## 1. Thesis

The Standing Repo Facility drawing $5 billion days after quarter-end proves the SOFR squeeze is persistent funding stress rather than calendar noise: SOFR sits 17 basis points over the 4.00% RRP rate on a two-week average and above the 4.15% interest on reserves for a full month, even though only $10 billion remains in the RRP buffer and bank reserves already sagged below $3 trillion on a $570 billion TGA rebuild to $850 billion. The 2019 and March-2023 playbook says the Fed eventually prints reserves to buy collateral, which is why gold at an all-time high, silver's record weekly close, and Bitcoin ripping are framed as the bearer-asset hedge against both counterparty risk and the coming currency debasement.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] SOFR above administered rates means dollars are scarce because the free-market overnight price only exceeds policy rates when cash holders outnumber borrowers.
- [TAKE] Quarter-end cannot explain the move because the two-week and one-month averages stayed elevated days after quarter-end.
- [TAKE] The TGA rebuild plus an empty RRP drains reserves because $570 billion of bill supply must be absorbed by the $2.9 trillion reserve tank.
- [TAKE] Banks parking at 4.15% IORB over higher SOFR reveals counterparty fear because risk-free Fed reserves beat riskier overnight lending by 2 basis points on trillions.
- [TAKE] Eventual Fed reserve printing to buy securities resolves funding stress but dilutes dollars, which is why gold, silver, and scarce Bitcoin hedge the debasement.
