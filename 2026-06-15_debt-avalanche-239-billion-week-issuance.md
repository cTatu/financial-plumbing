# The Debt Avalanche: $239 BILLION this Week Alone... 2026 Total Issuance will be WHAT?! Yes, Really.

## 1. Thesis

The Treasury sold $239B in one week but the headline flatters demand: the strong 10-year with 78.2% indirects and a 0.1bp stop-through contrasts with an ugly 30-year tail to 5.02% and the weakest foreign take since August 2025. Because activist issuance since Yellen concentrates supply in bills that must be rolled every 1-12 months, gross issuance already hit $13.1T through May and annualizes toward $30T — so any Fed hike that lifts the 3.3% average coupon on marketable debt directly widens the 12%-growth interest bill, the 5%-of-GDP deficit, and next week's auction size.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] Duration issuance is shrinking by design because activist bill-heavy supply lifts price and suppresses long-end yield when demand is held constant.
- [TAKE] The 10-year strength versus 30-year weakness shows structural duration buyers like pensions and insurers absorb some long bonds, but marginal foreign demand fades at 5% coupons.
- [TAKE] T-bill yields follow Fed funds almost tick-for-tick, so hiking the overnight rate immediately raises rollover cost on the $600B maturing every five days.
- [TAKE] Every point on the curve sits above the 3.3% average coupon, so all refinancing lifts the average rate and interest expense that is already compounding at 12%.
- [TAKE] The deficit must be funded from someone's excess savings, so a steady 1.6-1.7T deficit with an improving trade balance shifts absorption onto domestic private savings.

- [PREDICTS] Gross issuance tracks toward ~$30T in 2026 as bill share keeps accelerating rollover frequency. -> check: Treasury quarterly refunding and FYTD gross issuance; shift back to coupons slows the pace.
