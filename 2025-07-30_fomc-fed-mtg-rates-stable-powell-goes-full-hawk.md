# FOMC Fed Mtg: Rates Stable & Powell Goes Full Hawk

## 1. Thesis

The Fed held rates with two dissents for cuts while Powell sounded outright hawkish: 4.33% is only modestly restrictive, September is undecided until Jackson Hole, tariff pass-through to consumers will be small and one-time, and the market should be grateful hikes are off the table. Yields rose, gold and stocks faded, and the dollar squeezed hard against euros, francs, and yen — a short-term TGA-refill squeeze layered onto a hawkish surprise that unwinds part of the April de-dollarization rotation.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] [03:28] Tariff costs fall mostly on suppliers and retailers with only a small consumer share, so the lift is a one-time price-level adjustment that is the textbook case for transitory inflation.
- [TAKE] [03:43] Powell framing the hold as looking through goods inflation tells the market it should be thankful hikes are off the table, which is a deliberately hawkish signal.
- [TAKE] [07:53] An independent central bank serves the public well, so Powell keeps Fed and Treasury mandates separate even under direct White House pressure for cuts and renovations scrutiny.
- [TAKE] [08:44] The Fed does not set mortgage rates because 10-year, 20-year, and 30-year yields trade on fiscal, inflation, and growth risk, so durable housing relief requires lower inflation rather than funds-rate cuts.
- [TAKE] [11:53] Dollar strength concentrates in euros, yen, and francs that led the April rotation out of the US, so the squeeze reads as positioning unwind amplified by TGA-refill liquidity drain and higher-for-longer repricing.
