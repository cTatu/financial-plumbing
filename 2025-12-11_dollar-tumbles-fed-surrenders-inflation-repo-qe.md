# Dollar Tumbles as Fed Surrenders on Inflation & Announces More Money Printing to Save Repo Market

## 1. Thesis

The FOMC cut 25bp, removed the $500B SRF cap, and restarted ~$40B-per-month bill buying to relieve repo stress — functionally QE for overnight rates — while inflation sits at 3%, 55 months above target, and assets sit at highs. The market read it as surrender: DXY fell almost 1% in 12 hours, the 10-year rose from 3.6% to 4.1% since cuts began, and gold's 114% run since 2022 prices the devaluation. Easing into easy financial conditions with a higher GDP and lower unemployment/inflation forecast locks in fiscal dominance where nominal growth must exceed debt cost.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] RMPs are functionally QE because printing reserves to suppress short rates repeats the QE mechanism of printing to suppress long rates.
- [TAKE] Cuts cannot fix repo tightness that stems from willingness-to-lend and collateral plumbing, which is why elevated SOFR spreads demanded reserve injection.
- [TAKE] Too-dovish easing lifts the 10-year because nominal-growth and inflation expectations reprice higher, so mortgage, auto, and card rates tied to the 10-year worsen for households.
- [TAKE] The 1940s template shows the exit because 22% nominal growth with ~10% inflation held debt cost below growth and inflated the burden away.
- [TAKE] Gold prices fiscal dominance in advance because a higher gold price is arithmetically a lower dollar measured in ounces.
