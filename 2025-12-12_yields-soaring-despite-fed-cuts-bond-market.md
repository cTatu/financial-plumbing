# Bond Yields are Soaring, DESPITE Fed Rate Cuts - What is Going on in the Bond Market?

## 1. Thesis

Cuts to overnight funds do not cut what households pay because cards, autos, and mortgages price off the 10-year, now 55bp above funds for the widest gap in three years. The long end reflects nominal-growth expectations plus term premium plus fiscal supply: deficits near 6% of GDP with no war or recession, $1.16T in yearly interest consuming 23% of revenue, and a global duration rout push yields up even as the Fed eases. The attempted fix — steeper curve for banks, bill-heavy funding, and negative real rates — is financial repression that devalues bondholders to stabilize debt-to-GDP.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] Funds cuts subsidize banks first because a steeper curve lets intermediaries borrow short and lend long while household rates follow the 10-year higher.
- [TAKE] Mortgage spreads stay sticky because the 30-year-minus-10-year premium rides bond volatility, and MOVE remains elevated versus pre-2020 history.
- [TAKE] December divergence reflects growth repricing because fewer priced cuts plus lower unemployment and higher inflation lift nominal-growth expectations in the long end.
- [TAKE] The doom loop is mechanical because higher rates lift interest, interest lifts deficits, deficits lift bond supply, and supply into flat demand lifts yields again.
- [TAKE] Bill-funded repression is the prescribed exit because zero front rates with 3-4% inflation engineer negative real yields that liquidate debt through bondholders.

## 4C Predicts

- [PREDICTS] Mortgage and card rates stay high even if funds fall further so long as 10Y-funds holds wide. -> check: 10Y-funds near 55bp with mortgage premium near 200bp confirms it; spread collapsing toward zero with MOVE normalizing refutes it.
- [PREDICTS] More bill-heavy funding plus funds cuts steepen the curve without lowering long yields. -> check: bills lower with 10Y/30Y flat-to-higher confirms it; 10Y/30Y falling with bills says supply relief works.
