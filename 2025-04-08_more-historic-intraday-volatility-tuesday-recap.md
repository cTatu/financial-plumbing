# More Historic Intraday Volatility - Tuesday April 8th Markets Recap

## 1. Thesis

Tuesday confirmed the scare is in duration, not just equities: another 6%-plus intraday equity swing ended red while long-end yields broke out to the upside into the largest tariff shock in a century. The 2-year rallied as the 10-, 20-, and 30-year sold off, steepening the curve on fiscal and foreign-selling fear while a record-weak 3-year auction and a 139 MOVE print signaled dysfunction normally reserved for historic bond bears. With stocks, bonds, and the dollar falling together in repeated intraday windows and haven flow favoring the franc and yen, the tape increasingly resembles the early stages of an emerging-market-style rates-up-in-a-downturn dynamic.
