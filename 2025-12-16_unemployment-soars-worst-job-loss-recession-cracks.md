# Unemployment Soars, Worst Job Loss in 5yrs - Imminent Recession? Labor Market Cracks?

## 1. Thesis

The delayed November report looks soft on the surface — +64K payrolls, 4.6% unemployment — but the internals show a cracking labor market: October's -105K is the worst print in five years, short-term joblessness is surging, 5.5M work part-time for economic reasons, and 13-14M are functionally unemployed once the marginally attached and discouraged are counted. With multiple jobholders and part-time at all-time highs, youth and Black unemployment spiking as classic leading indicators, and wages flat in gold and S&P terms, the read is late-cycle K-shaped deterioration where half of GDP rides a handful of AI-CapEx names while workers absorb stagnation.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] [03:39] The headline 4.6% understates slack because U6 counts the part-time-for-economic-reasons and marginally attached workers that U3 excludes, running near double the headline rate.
- [TAKE] [04:19] Gains concentrate in healthcare, social assistance, and construction specialty trades while manufacturing sheds payrolls, which is not the profile of a healthy expansion.
- [TAKE] [06:54] From 2018-2024 not one full-time job went to native-born Americans, and only negative net migration plus a collapsed 20-25K breakeven rate now flatter the prints versus the old 120-140K bar.
- [TAKE] [12:52] Youth and Black unemployment surging ahead of U3 is the classic leading sequence, so the four-year-high headline reads as pre-recession rather than noise.
- [TAKE] [13:22] GDP is K-shaped because roughly half of output rides five AI-CapEx companies, so aggregate growth can hold while typical workers face wage stagnation and job loss.
