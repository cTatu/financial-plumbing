# Why a Skyrocketing Gold Price Should Scare You - the Denominator is Breaking

## 1. Thesis

Gold ripping from $1,800 to $3,500 is not wealth creation but a ruler stretching: narrow money grew about 11,500% since 1950 against gold's about 11,090%, so an ounce still buys the same scarcity while pricing in dollars buys less. Flipping the denominator exposes the illusion — the S&P at new highs in dollars sits well below highs in euros, francs, and especially gold, with stocks and homes both far cheaper in ounces than at the dot-com peak. The market is forward-pricing a further loss of dollar purchasing power driven by developed-world fiscal gaps, which is why even committed dollar bulls now warn about the denominator failing.
