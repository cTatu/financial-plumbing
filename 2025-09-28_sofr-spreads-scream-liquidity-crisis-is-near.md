# Risk of a Financial Crisis is Soaring - SOFR Spreads Scream a Liquidity Crisis is Near

## 1. Thesis

Dollar funding is seizing outside any calendar excuse: the 14-day and 28-day SOFR-minus-RRP averages sit at their widest since March 2020 even though the September 15 tax date is long past, because QT plus a $300B-to-$750B TGA rebuild has drained reserves to $3T (13% of M2) with only $48B left in the RRP buffer. That scarcity is already priced across markets — Bitcoin topped September 18 as the first thing sold, yields rise across 2Y/10Y/30Y in a one-to-one 10-day lockstep with the spread, the VIX flashes a 20-over-50-day trend cross, and quarter-end in two days lands on an empty tank with a 7%-of-GDP deficit still demanding $2T a year.

- [TAKE] The SOFR-minus-administered-rate spread measures Fed control because free-market dollar cost above the policy rate means scarce dollars command a premium.
- [TAKE] Calendar effects cannot explain the move because tightness built for a month outside month-end and quarter-end with only the long-past September 15 tax date nearby.
- [TAKE] Tighter liquidity lifts long yields mechanically because fewer dollars remain available to absorb roughly $2 trillion per year of new Treasury supply.
- [TAKE] QT plus the TGA rebuild compounds the drain because destroyed reserves cannot be replaced once the $48 billion RRP buffer is exhausted.
- [TAKE] Risk assets confirm funding stress because Bitcoin topped September 18 while yields rose, the dollar spiked, the VIX crossed up, and the S&P sold off together.
