# Quarter of a Trillion in Debt Sold Today Alone - Auction Results, Fiscal Dominance & Why it Matters

## 1. Thesis

The Treasury sold almost $220B in one day — $86B of 3-month plus $77B of 6-month bills with Fed add-ons plus $58B of 3-years at 3.61%, the highest since August — yet internals were firm with a fourth straight 0.8bp stop-through, 2.64 cover, and only 9% dealer takedown. Strength misses the point: concentrating funding in 1-3 month paper that must be rolled constantly removes the Fed's freedom to fight a 3%-CPI inflation impulse, because any hike reprices the stock immediately into interest, deficit, and still more supply. That is fiscal dominance as financial repression — 1940s playbook with 15%-plus inflation — where bills act as leverable money and negative real yields plus gold are the intended release valve.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] Bills clear easily because a 3-month promise is an interest-bearing dollar with low volatility and full-faith backing, which is why money funds absorb them.
- [TAKE] Bill concentration destroys Fed independence because rolling the stock every 1-3 months turns any anti-inflation hike into instant interest and deficit expansion.
- [TAKE] The 1940s playbook is the template because 120% debt-to-GDP was held serviceable through capped yields and 15%-plus inflation rather than repayment.
- [TAKE] Bills monetize more than bonds because low haircuts lever readily into private credit while pension-bought long bonds cannot create dollars.
- [TAKE] A 1% bill yield against 3% CPI is deliberate repression because bondholders absorb the loss that keeps Treasury interest serviceable.

## 4C Predicts

- [PREDICTS] Negative real yields return with gold outperforming as bills-plus-repression debase the holder. -> check: 3-month near 1% with CPI near 3% plus gold strength confirms it; bills above CPI with gold weak refutes it.
