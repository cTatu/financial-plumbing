## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] Nominal retail and wage prints mislead because 4.30% growth means little when the price level absorbs most of it, leaving 1.30% real growth.
- [TAKE] Households experience inflation as the cumulative 38.00% rise in the price level since 2020, not the 2.70% year-over-year rate economists quote.
- [TAKE] The price level only falls in contraction, so public anger over cumulative costs and the cost of fixing them through job loss is the administration's bind.
- [TAKE] Producers feel inflation first in ISM prices, then in PPI, then consumers feel it in CPI months later, which makes the ISM uptrend since 2023 a forward warning.
- [TAKE] Spending per worker is a more direct inflation read than hedonic CPI because it measures dollars actually leaving household budgets even before volume adjustment.
