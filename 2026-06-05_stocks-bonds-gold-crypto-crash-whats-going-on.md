# Stocks, Bonds, Gold & Crypto Crash - What's Going On?

## 1. Thesis

A hot May payrolls print repriced the Fed from cuts to hikes and detonated a crowded positioning bubble: everything sold together because record dispersion, 5-year extremes in call buying, and roughly 40% index weight in a handful of AI names had left equities dependent on dealer gamma support while bonds already priced fiscal-dominance risk. With the 2-year at its highest since February 2025, the 1-year pricing a near-certain hike, and the earnings yield below the 10-year, the cross-asset dump plus a bear flattener is the discount-rate shock meeting debt-funded AI capex and soaring tech credit stress.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] The payroll beat plus upward revision flipped the 2-year above Fed funds, so the bond market now prices hikes that tighten financial conditions into a debt-rollover machine.
- [TAKE] Record-low implied correlation plus record call skew created a gamma squeeze where dealer hedging pushed a narrow AI cohort higher while most stocks already lagged.
- [TAKE] Concentration near 40% in AI names with financials versus tech near all-time lows leaves the index vulnerable to simultaneous unwinds in global pairs like KOSPI and Taiwan.
- [TAKE] Debt-funded hyperscaler capex that exceeds total infrastructure spending plus soaring CDS and circular other-income marks a self-referential loop once free cash flow turns negative.
- [TAKE] Negative equity risk premium plus 2.5% real 10-year yields punish long-duration equities, gold, and Bitcoin together when the discount rate jumps.
