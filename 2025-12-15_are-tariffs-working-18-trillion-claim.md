# Are Tariffs Working? Did We Really 'Take in' $18 TRILLION DOLLARS as Trump Claims?

## 1. Thesis

The September deficit did narrow to $52.8B, the best in over five years, but the improvement is gold and pharma accounting plus post-front-running normalization — not reshoring — while import prices at +0.3% YoY show no tariff inflation pass-through. The $18T "taken in" is verbal FDI pledges rather than collected revenue, and duties cannot fix the balance by themselves because Triffin overvaluation of the dollar makes US production uneconomic; without a Plaza-style dollar reset, the $1.3T current-account deficit and -90%-of-GDP NIIP keep the country selling assets to fund consumption.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] September narrowing is gold and pharma noise because $6.1B of the $7.2B industrial-supplies gain is gold exports that a twin-deficit nation cannot accumulate.
- [TAKE] Front-running distorts the signal because firms surged imports ahead of duties and the current calm needs a couple of quarters to settle.
- [TAKE] Tariff inflation is absent in the data because flat import prices plus only 15-25bp of goods CPI contribution contradict the surge narrative.
- [TAKE] The $18T figure is verbal FDI pledges rather than cash collected, and FDI is only one way to finance the $1.3T current-account deficit.
- [TAKE] The dollar is the binding constraint because PPP overvaluation above 1985 Plaza levels plus one-sixth Chinese energy costs makes US production uneconomic at current FX.
