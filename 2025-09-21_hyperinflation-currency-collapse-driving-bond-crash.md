# Hyperinflation & Currency Collapse - What's Driving the Bond Market Crash?

## 1. Thesis

Argentina is the cleanest demo of the denominator: gold priced in pesos is up ~6,000% since 2022 not because gold got more valuable but because the peso buys that much less, so stocks, houses, and money-supply trillions all go parabolic in peso terms. The structure behind it is a twin deficit where a near-record export boom is still dragged into current-account deficit by primary-income interest owed abroad plus an 8.3%-of-GDP fiscal peak, and Milei's spending cuts plus 45% rates have cut inflation from 292% to 33% with growth back to 6.4% — yet bonds at 40-50 cents on the dollar and a bear-flattening selloff after local election losses show the market still prices a return to print-and-spend.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] Scarce assets rise in peso terms because the unit of account purchases less, not because houses, stocks, or gold became more productive.
- [TAKE] A trade surplus still produces a current-account deficit when primary-income interest on external debt exceeds the goods surplus.
- [TAKE] Tax hikes on a fiscally stressed state cut revenue as a share of GDP because they suppress the growth that funds receipts and push mobile capital offshore.
- [TAKE] Money printing drives the price level because M2 growing 200% per year arithmetically precedes 292% inflation with a lag.
- [TAKE] The bond selloff to 50 cents with short-end yields spiking is a bear flattener driven by fears that austerity loses politically and profligate spending returns.
