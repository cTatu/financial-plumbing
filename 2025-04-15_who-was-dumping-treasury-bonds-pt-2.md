# Who Was Dumping US Treasury Bonds - Pt 2

## 1. Thesis

Part 2 extends the rate-differential test to Canada and the United Kingdom: both the Canadian dollar and the pound strengthen while their differentials fall, at the same early-Thursday moment the US 10-year sells off. The video then rejects Bessent's no-foreign-selling read and the domestic-only deleveraging story by pairing that synchronized break with a generational DXY-versus-yield divergence and a 3%-in-two-days dollar dump. The deeper claim is that America built the dollar into the global carry trade by recycling $100-billion-plus monthly deficits into its own markets, so balanced-trade rhetoric plus a Mar-a-Lago-style reordering reads as an intentional end to price-insensitive foreign demand, with gold's 60% run as the market pricing the fiat consequence.
