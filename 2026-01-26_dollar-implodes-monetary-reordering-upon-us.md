# Dollar Implodes — A Monetary Reordering Is Upon Us

## 1. Thesis

The dollar's largest five-day plunge since Liberation Day, alongside BoJ and suspected NY Fed rate checks, is framed not as a tactical FX squeeze but as confirmation that the reserve-currency flywheel is reversing toward a Mar-a-Lago / Plaza 2.0 reordering. With the trade deficit supposedly shrinking yet the dollar still falling, Japan sitting on $1.3T of reserves, gold over $5,000 and silver over $100 with a $15 Shanghai premium plus spot backwardation, the argument is that de-dollarization flows now sell dollars to buy gold as the neutral pivot, forcing a balance-of-payments revaluation of an overvalued dollar against deeply undervalued yen and yuan over years — or abruptly through an official accord.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] A rate check works because the quoted bank front-runs the central bank and traders spread the rumor, so the market does the intervention work for free.
- [TAKE] The Treasury sets US currency policy and the New York Fed merely executes it, so a Fed rate check against the dollar implies Treasury direction to help Japan.
- [TAKE] Gold rising above $5,000 does not mean gold changed in value but means the market is pricing destruction of dollar purchasing power, since dollars carry 3.25% yield and gold carries none.
- [TAKE] The dollar flywheel depended on foreigners earning dollars from the trade deficit and recycling them into US assets plus dollar strength, so shrinking deficits plus dollar weakness breaks the carry in reverse.
- [TAKE] Gold freely floating in all currencies is positioned as the neutral pivot that revalues yen, yuan, and dollars on balance-of-payments grounds with or without a formal Mar-a-Lago accord.
