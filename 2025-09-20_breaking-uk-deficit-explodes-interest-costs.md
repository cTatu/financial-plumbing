# Breaking: UK Deficit EXPLODES Higher - Interest Costs Skyrocketing from Bond Meltdown

## 1. Thesis

August UK borrowing printed £18 billion versus £12 billion expected, up 24% year-over-year and the worst August since 2020, confirming a textbook debt doom loop where more supply lifts gilt yields, interest expense near £95 billion then lifts the deficit further. Tax hikes are accelerating the spiral rather than closing it as revenue slides from 28% to 25% of GDP while wealthy taxpayers exit, and with 15-year gilts near 5.1% above 5% nominal growth against 3.8% inflation and 1.2% real growth, the UK sits in stagflation where only enforced low rates or high inflation can reconcile the arithmetic.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] [01:30] Higher deficits force more gilt supply that lowers bond values and lifts yields, and higher yields lift interest expense that lifts the deficit again.
- [TAKE] [03:19] Raising taxes in a debt crisis shrinks the base because mobile top taxpayers flee and remaining activity slows, so revenue falls as a share of GDP.
- [TAKE] [04:19] Tariffs act as demand-side taxes because they remove spendable income and constrain consumption-led growth.
- [TAKE] [12:20] Issuing in 3-month bills to chase low front-end rates destroys monetary independence because any hike immediately reprices the entire stock.
- [TAKE] [18:20] Nominal growth is the only fiscal variable that matters because 1.2% real plus 3.8% inflation still leaves the government short of the 6.5% stabilizer.
