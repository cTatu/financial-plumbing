# Worst Housing Market on Record - Why it'll Only get Worse

## 1. Thesis

Affordability is not a shortage of wooden boxes but a bond-market outcome: unit counts outran population while rates repriced the mortgage leg, tripling yearly interest and leaving a low-liquidity market where wealthy cash buyers set marginal prices. With the rate downtrend likely over, Fed MBS subsidy embedded, and M2 at new highs chasing flat construction, the base case is sideways-to-higher national prices on collapsing volume rather than a crash — which only forced selling or full Fed exit would break.

- [23:24] The Fed cut the federal funds rate by 100 basis points while the 30-year Treasury yield rose by 100 basis points. (as of current cutting cycle to Jul 2025)
- [27:00] The Fed owns 17% of residential mortgages versus 0% before the GFC, or about $2 trillion of $12.8 trillion in residential mortgages. (as of Jul 2025 Fed H.4.1)

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] [07:25] Houses did not get more valuable because the fall from 800 to 150 ounces proves the dollar denominator failed while the structure stayed the same.
- [TAKE] [11:52] Annual interest matters more than sticker price because financing a 40% higher price at much higher rates for 30 years produced the $8,000-to-$27,000 spike.
- [TAKE] [20:42] Boomers could refinance for 40 years into falling rates while current buyers cannot because the multi-decade rate downtrend has broken to the upside.
- [TAKE] [23:24] Fed cuts do not lower mortgage rates when the long end prices fiscal risk because the 100bp cut with 100bp higher 30-year produced a bear steepener.
- [TAKE] [27:00] Fed MBS holdings subsidize housing risk because printing reserves to buy bonds lifts bond values and suppresses mortgage rates below free-market levels.
