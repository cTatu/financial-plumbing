# Gold Hits $4,000, Reverse Repo Hits Lowest Level in 4yrs, $58bln Bond Auction

## 1. Thesis

Gold breaking $4,000 while the reverse-repo tank sits at $4.6 billion is one story about confidence in paper: investors accept zero-yield bearer money over interest-bearing dollars because the yield no longer compensates for debasement and seizure risk. The same session's $58 billion 3-year auction still stopped through by 0.8 basis points with 2.66 cover, but foreign share slipped to 62.7% versus a 64.1% six-auction average while Treasury queues a $110 billion 4-week bill plus $39 billion 10-year and $22 billion 30-year. With the TGA rebuilt from about $300 billion to $820 billion and reserves below $3 trillion into ongoing QT, each bill must be funded from reserves — which is why SOFR-minus-RRP at 15 to 24 basis points reads as tightening, not noise, and why the global 30-year selloff keeps pressing the interest-deficit-supply spiral.

- [TAKE] The global 30-year selloff drives the debt spiral mechanically because higher yields lift interest expense, deficits, and future bond supply.
