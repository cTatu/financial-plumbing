# Government Shutdown - Economic & Market Effects

## 1. Thesis

Shutdowns look scary into the event but the tape says the opposite: across four prior episodes the dollar slipped about 0.6% on average while the S&P 500 rallied about 3%, the 10-year picked up a 3bp haven bid, and gold added 0.7%, because forward-looking markets price the disruption before it lands. The real risk this time is slower-burning — Moody's 0.1-0.2pp of quarterly GDP lost per week off a 4% Q3 base compounds fast over a month-long stoppage, and a Trump plan to convert 200,000-300,000 furloughs into permanent cuts would turn a statistical unemployment blip the Fed can look through into a genuine labor-market loosening that pulls rate cuts forward.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] Shutdown markets run buy-the-rumor-sell-the-news because prices discount the disruption in advance, which is why the 2018 month-long partial stoppage still saw the S&P 500 rally 7% while the dollar fell.
- [TAKE] Temporary furloughs are statistical noise the Fed can look through since counted-as-unemployed workers reverse on reopening, whereas converting a quarter-million cuts permanent would be a genuine loosening the Fed must answer with lower rates.
- [TAKE] A month-long stoppage matters even from a 4% base because four weekly 0.1-0.2pp hits remove nearly a full point and leave growth closer to 3%.
- [TAKE] The current tape already shows the haven pattern with bonds bid across the curve, gold ripping, Bitcoin soft, and the VIX near 16.2 pricing shutdown volatility.
