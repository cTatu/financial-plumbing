# Dollar Liquidity Nightmare Gets Worse - SOFR-FFR Hits 5-yr Intra-month Highs

## 1. Thesis

Overnight dollar funding is freezing outside any calendar excuse: SOFR has held above fed funds since last Tuesday to a five-year intra-month high, a persistence last seen before the September 2019 repo blowout. With the Treasury still needing $200 billion to rebuild the TGA to $850 billion and the reverse-repo tank at $19 billion versus $2.5 trillion in 2022, the refill must come from $3.1 trillion of reserves already $1 trillion lighter from QT. Reserves sliding toward 13% of M2 repeats the exact thin-cover setup that preceded three of the four largest US bank failures in early 2023, which is why talk of a 50-basis-point cut into all-time-high equities and gold reads as plumbing stress rather than macro insurance.

- [TAKE] [05:10] Persistence makes this episode different because month-end spikes normally reverse in a day or two while this spread has held since last Tuesday.
- [TAKE] [11:28] TGA rebuilds drain risk assets because refilling the government's checking account from reserves removes dollars that would otherwise fund financial assets.
- [TAKE] [13:50] The 13% reserves-to-M2 line matters because the last visit in January 2023 came weeks before three of the four largest US bank failures.
- [TAKE] [17:42] An imminent 50-basis-point cut into all-time-high equities, homes, and gold signals funding stress rather than easing room because financial conditions are already looser than at the start of 2022 hikes.
- [TAKE] [12:05] QT creates the shortage mechanically because every Treasury refill without an RRP buffer must destroy further reserves while M2 keeps growing.
