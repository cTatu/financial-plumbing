# Just In: Trump Floats 50 Year Mortgage - Solution, or Nightmare?

## 1. Thesis

The floated 50-year mortgage (with FHA confirmation via Pulte) cuts the monthly payment at the cost of nearly doubling lifetime interest while leaving every structural driver untouched: it stimulates more private-bank mortgage credit against scarce housing so prices rise, demands roughly a 1-point duration and negative-convexity premium that private buyers will not absorb at a normal curve, and therefore can only clear with a federal backstop or Fed balance-sheet support. With 7%-of-GDP deficits holding rates up, bank card spreads at 18% over the 10-year, the Fed still holding $2 trillion of MBS, and a $1-trillion yearly external bid into US assets, the proposal converts an affordability crisis into a larger debt and volatility load rather than a fix.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] A 50-year term lowers the monthly payment by stretching amortization, so qualification expands while lifetime interest nearly doubles on the same house.
- [TAKE] More qualification means more bank mortgage credit, and because banks print dollars when they lend, new dollars chase scarce houses and lift prices.
- [TAKE] Fifty-year MBS carry longer duration plus stronger negative convexity from prepayment risk, so investors demand about 1 point over the 30-year rate.
- [TAKE] The private math fails at a 100bp curve pickup, so the product only clears if the government guarantees or the Fed buys the extended MBS.
- [TAKE] The plan fixes none of the level causes because 7%-of-GDP deficits, 18% bank spreads, $2 trillion of Fed MBS subsidy, and $1-trillion external asset bidding all remain.

## 4C Predicts

- [PREDICTS] A 50-year product prices about 100bp over the 30-year mortgage on convexity and duration. -> check: announced 50Y rate sheet near +100bp over 30Y confirms it; pricing near 30Y flat refutes private clearing.
- [PREDICTS] Authorization spurs mortgage-credit growth and pushes home prices higher on new demand. -> check: mortgage-debt growth reaccelerating with prices up confirms it; volumes flat with prices soft refutes stimulus.
- [PREDICTS] Clearing requires federal guarantee or Fed MBS absorption with higher MOVE pass-through. -> check: FHA guarantee language or Fed MBS buying with 30Y-10Y spread wider confirms it; purely private issuance at tight spreads refutes it.
