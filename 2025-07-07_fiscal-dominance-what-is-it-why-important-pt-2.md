# Fiscal Dominance: What is it & why it's important -- Pt 2

## 1. Thesis

Part 2 turns the Calamiris paper into a two-path playbook: ending interest on reserves widens the inflation-tax base from $2.39T of currency to $5.60T of base money but kills the $141B yearly bank subsidy, collapses private credit, and forces a 1930s-style deflationary spiral that cannot survive with 125% debt-to-GDP and M2 at 4x base — so it must be answered within months by trillions in money printing. Keeping 4.40% IORB above bill yields avoids that banking shock but leaves the base too small to fund deficits, which points to the other path of 10% to 20% sustained inflation where long bonds lose in real terms, scarcity wins, and Trump's 300-basis-point cut call plus all-short issuance is already the emerging-market tell.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] Deficits doubling while unemployment improved from 5% to 4% breaks the postwar rule that deficits heal with jobs.
- [TAKE] Raising taxes as a share of GDP lowers real growth and widens deficit-to-GDP because the denominator shrinks faster than revenue rises.
- [TAKE] Rolling 3.30% average debt into a curve with nothing below that rate mechanically lifts interest and forces more supply with steady demand.
- [TAKE] Keeping 4.40% IORB sterilizes $3.20T of reserves and proves banks will not lend the base the government needs to tax.
- [TAKE] Forcing zero-rate reserves on 40% of $18T deposits would crush bank profits, kill private credit creation, and trigger a deflationary spiral that highly levered collateral cannot survive.

## 4C Predicts

- [PREDICTS] High 10%-to-20% inflation dominates over sustained deflation because any reserve-shock lending collapse is met with trillions in printing within months. -> check: CPI persistence with IORB intact versus private-loan contraction with zero-rate requirements; outright deflation with no fiscal response refutes it.
- [PREDICTS] Long bonds keep losing in real terms while scarce assets lead, with stocks up nominally but down in gold or CPI terms. -> check: TLT divided by CPI and gold plus S&P divided by gold over next year; TLT beating gold and CPI refutes it.
- [PREDICTS] The curve bear-steepens as short issuance plus 300bp cut talk lifts longs, ending in Operation Twist–style short-funded long-end buying. -> check: 30-year-minus-3-month spread with bill share and Fed maturity buys; bull flattening with falling longs refutes it.

## 7. Follow-ups

- Watch IORB with reserve-requirement talk: holding 4.40% with zero requirements confirms the high-inflation path; cutting toward zero with a 40% requirement confirms the bank-profit shock and deflation-first path.
- Watch marketable-debt average rate versus new-issue yields: average climbing above 3.30% with bills dominating issuance confirms the rollover ratchet; average stabilizing with 10-year back below 3.30% argues relief.
- Watch real TLT versus KRE with a 37-week lag: new real TLT lows followed by KRE weakness confirms the balance-sheet contagion; both rising together refutes it.
