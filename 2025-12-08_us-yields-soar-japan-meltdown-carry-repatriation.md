# US Bond Yields Soar - Japanese Bond Market Meltdown - Yen Carry Trade, Capital Repatriation Event?

## 1. Thesis

The US long-end selloff is imported as much as domestic: Japan's 10-year JGB near 2% for the first time in decades narrows the 2.5% two-year and 2.2% ten-year pickup on US paper over Japanese paper, undermining a multi-trillion yen-carry machine built on negative rates and a one-way weak yen. Japan's $1.1T of its $1.2T FX reserves in Treasuries plus trillions more in private dollar assets can flip from funding bid to sale — sell Treasuries, sell dollars, buy yen — whether through MoF intervention near 156 or voluntary repatriation into now-positive domestic yields, with the BoJ's 0.5% rate decision as the spark. Hassett trial-balloon politics then steepens the US curve from the other side via fiscal-dominance pricing.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] Surplus accounting forces Japan to be a creditor because a trade surplus must recycle into foreign-asset buying, which built the $1.1T Treasury bid.
- [TAKE] The carry stayed alive on free funding plus a weakening yen because negative rates and expected depreciation paid traders to borrow yen and buy dollars.
- [TAKE] Narrowing 2-year and 10-year premia erode the trade's edge because 2.5% and 2.2% of carry compensates less for FX risk than 3.3% did.
- [TAKE] Intervention and repatriation share one chain because selling Treasuries into selling dollars into buying yen and JGBs lifts US yields while dragging the dollar down.
- [TAKE] Hassett-as-chair chatter steepens from the US side because politicized-Fed plus fiscal-dominance pricing demands negative real yields that punish long bonds.

## 4C Predicts

- [PREDICTS] A BoJ hike from 0.5% rapidly strengthens the yen and unwinds the multi-trillion yen-carry position. -> check: yen sharply stronger with US long yields higher and Nikkei underperforming on the decision confirms unwind; yen weaker with US yields lower refutes it.
- [PREDICTS] Capital repatriation sells Treasuries and dollars to buy yen and JGBs, stabilizing JGB yields while US long yields keep rising. -> check: JGB yields steadying with US 30Y higher and USD/JPY lower confirms repatriation; JGB yields vertical with US 30Y falling refutes it.
