# 4-5% Inflation, AI Job Apocalypse and QE: Contender for Next Fed Chair Rieder Drops Bombshells

## 1. Thesis

BlackRock CIO Rick Rieder's Bloomberg audition for Fed chair sketches a run-it-hot regime: tolerate 3% inflation without alarm, treat 4-5% as the real line, cut the funds rate because the neutral rate sits lower, and use QE balance-sheet expansion to force the 10-year and 30-year down because everything from mortgages to credit cards prices off the long end. The presenter agrees on the mechanics but warns the distributional bet fails — AI and robotics structurally replace labor rather than upgrading it, activist Treasury issuance already suppresses duration at about $30 billion per 30-year auction, and negative real rates plus QE repeat post-WWII financial repression that helps scarce neutral assets while the lower-K leg faces 6-7% real inflation.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] [00:44] Labor softness is secular rather than cyclical because AI and robotics are designed to replace humans instead of moving them into higher-paid roles.
- [TAKE] [04:31] The Fed funds rate is a limited tool because households and firms borrow off the 10-year yield for mortgages, commercial real estate, credit cards, and auto loans.
- [TAKE] [06:02] Post-WWII financial repression worked by capping the 10-year below 16-18% inflation so nominal GDP outran borrowing costs and debt-to-GDP fell.
- [TAKE] [10:37] Activist Treasury issuance suppresses long yields mechanically because trimming $30 billion of 30-year supply against $30 billion of pension demand lifts price and lowers yield.
- [TAKE] [12:44] Running the economy hot helps gold and scarce neutral assets first because debasement plus reordering demand favors bearers that float in all currencies over nominal bonds.

## 4C Predicts

- [PREDICTS] The long end keeps grinding higher unless QE or issuance cuts intervene, holding above the 3.9% September-2024 starting point. -> check: 30Y above 4.6% with 10Y-Fed spread wide confirms it; 30Y back below 3.9% on cuts alone refutes it.
- [PREDICTS] A run-hot regime with 3-5% measured inflation leaves gold outperforming bonds while equities stay supported in nominal terms. -> check: gold up with negative-to-low real rates and SPX firm confirms it; gold fading as real rates rise refutes it.
