# Is Inflation being Undercounted by the Government?

## 1. Thesis

As a direct follow-up to the inflation-status video, he made this one to prove the official CPI understates lived debasement by comparing the index to money itself: over 2000-2025 the CPI compounded at 2.5% while M2 compounded at 6% and bank credit at 5.6%, leaving a 148% and 98% gap that flips the S&P's parabolic nominal gain into a loss versus M2 and a 69% loss versus gold. With deglobalization, less immigration, and an unsustainable fiscal position reversing decades of disinflation, the undercount matters because the next regime is higher sustained inflation and rates.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] [04:53] Inflation is a monetary phenomenon proportional to money creation, so 6% M2 and 5.6% credit growth versus 2.5% CPI proves systematic understatement.
- [TAKE] [05:26] Most dollars are private bank credit loaned into existence, with over 80% of money as bank loans rather than government-issued cash.
- [TAKE] [15:05] Gold supply grows about 1.8% per year, so its relative scarcity makes gold a stable denominator for real returns.
- [TAKE] [19:52] Over-indebted sovereigns deleverage through productivity gains or inflation, so the fiscal position points to inflating the debt away.
- [TAKE] [20:28] Reversing globalization, free trade, and imported cheap labor removes decades of disinflation and points to higher sustained rates.
