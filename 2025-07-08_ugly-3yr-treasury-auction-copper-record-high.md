# Ugly 3yr Treasury Auction and Copper Hits Record High

## 1. Thesis

The $58B 3-year sale tailed with only 2 stop-throughs since the September 2024 cut cycle began, and the internals split bullish from bearish: a record 29.40% direct take with falling dealer share says domestic funds cleared the bonds, while softer bid-to-cover since 2012-2013 and weak 54% indirects say foreign demand keeps fading. That matters more at 3 years than at 30 years because short-end liquidity is deeper and duration is thinner, so any price concession signals genuine softness. With the 30-year back at 4.97% near 5% on weaker nominal growth than 2023 or early 2025, copper at a $5.50 record after a 50% tariff headline, and 10-year yields rising together across 7 developed markets since July 3, the tape reads as a global bear steepener where 300bp of funds-cut talk plus all-short issuance lifts longs until short-funded long-end buying caps them.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] A tail means Treasury cut price and lifted yield to clear the full $58.00 billion, so the sale funded above the when-issued yield and signals soft demand.
- [TAKE] A 1.00-basis-point tail on the 3-year matters more than on the 30-year because deeper liquidity and lower duration should need almost no concession.
- [TAKE] Record 29.40% direct take with falling dealer share is bullish on domestic absorption, while 54.00% indirects with falling cover since 2013 is bearish on foreign depth.
- [TAKE] Returning to 5.00% on the 30-year with lower nominal growth than late 2023 or early 2025 tightens debt arithmetic because coupons exceed the growth funding them.
- [TAKE] Cutting funds 300.00 basis points to 1.00% at 4.10% unemployment while funding at the short end is emerging-market stimulus that bear-steepens until Twist-style long-end buying caps rates.
