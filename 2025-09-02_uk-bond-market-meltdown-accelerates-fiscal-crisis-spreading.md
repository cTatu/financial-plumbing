# UK Bond Market Meltdown Accelerates - The UK Fiscal Crisis is Spreading

## 1. Thesis

Britain prints the textbook emerging-market sequence inside a developed market: the 30-year gilt spiked to a 27-year high while the pound fell 1% on the day, meaning foreign buyers are not arriving to clip higher yield and domestic holders are selling bonds and currency together. The driver is a twin deficit with 5% fiscal gap and 57%-of-GDP spending against 1.2% real growth and 3.6% inflation, so each point of yield adds to interest already at 85B pounds and forces the doom-loop issuance that transmits through $858B of UK-held Treasuries into US yields while gold up 2% and a soft dollar confirm neutral-asset bid.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] Rising yield with a falling pound is capital flight because textbook buying would sell foreign currency for pounds, so joint weakness means holders exit both bonds and currency.
- [TAKE] The bear steepener diagnoses fiscal stress because short yields fall on cut expectations while 2-year through 50-year yields rise on inflation and supply compensation.
- [TAKE] The doom loop is arithmetic because a 5%-of-GDP deficit forces matching issuance, added supply lowers bond values and lifts yields, and higher interest widens the deficit into more supply.
- [TAKE] Inflation-linked debt cuts both ways because disinflation from 14% to 5% once lowered service while the rebound from 1.7% to 3.6% now raises it on a quarter of the stock.
- [TAKE] UK stress transmits to Treasuries because a twin-deficit holder of $858B sells what it can rather than what it wants, so gilt liquidation becomes Treasury supply.
