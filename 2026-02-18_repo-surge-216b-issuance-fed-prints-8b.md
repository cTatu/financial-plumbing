# Repo Use Surges Amid $216 Billion of Treasury Bond Issuance, Fed Prints $8 Billion - What's Going On?

## 1. Thesis

Standing repo drew $30.5B — the third-largest use since 2020 with an unusual $19B afternoon leg — while SOFR spreads widened even though the market is nowhere near month- or quarter-end. The episode ties the squeeze to $216B of T-bill settlements landing after QT plus TGA rebuild drained reserves toward $2.9T, which matters because a 50-100x levered basis trade absorbs about half of new Treasury supply; the Fed's answer is already-visible $8B of reserve-management bill buying that lifts reserves instead of duration, a short-end patch that eases plumbing without fixing bill dependence.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] Afternoon SRF use plus widening SOFR away from quarter-end proves a cash shortage rather than window-dressing, because afternoon demand is normally quiet.
- [TAKE] QT plus TGA rebuild had to come from reserves once RRP emptied, so $2.9T reserves with negative RRP-minus-SRF is the mechanical source of stress.
- [TAKE] The Fed restarted money printing in December because SOFR above the bound threatened the basis trade that funds half the deficit, not because it wants QE duration.
- [TAKE] RMP bill buying can calm overnight plumbing by adding reserves, but funding $600B every 5 days in bills keeps the rollover trap intact.
- [TAKE] Yellen-style bill dependence removed Fed independence before politics did, because 1- to 6-month funding reprices instantly and punishes any hiking cycle with interest expense.
