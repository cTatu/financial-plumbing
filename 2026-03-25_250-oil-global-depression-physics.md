# $250 Oil? - A Global DEPRESSION is Coming, and it's Just Physics

## 1. Thesis

The Strait of Hormuz closure removes roughly 20 million barrels per day plus 8 million barrels per day of shut-ins against 105 million barrels per day of demand, a 24-million-barrel net supply shock that mirrors the 23-million-barrel demand collapse of Q2 2020. Because oil is physical and price-inelastic, the gap cannot be printed away and must clear through demand destruction, which implies $250 to $300 oil, a forced consumption collapse on the scale of a 32% annualized GDP contraction, and a funding chain where importers sell Treasuries and stocks, the dollar tightens, and the US deficit explodes toward Fed monetization.
