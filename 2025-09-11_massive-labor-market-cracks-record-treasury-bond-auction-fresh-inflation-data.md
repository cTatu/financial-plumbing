# Massive Labor Market Cracks + Record Treasury Bond Auction + Fresh Inflation Data

## 1. Thesis

Cracks and strength arrived the same morning: initial claims broke out to 263,000 with U6 double headline and 30-year to 2-year flattening on four cuts priced in five meetings, yet the $39 billion 10-year auction stopped through 1.3 basis points with 2.65 cover and record-low 4.2% dealer take on near-record foreign buying. CPI confirms the tension at 2.9% headline and 3.1% core with services driving 217 of 310 basis points while energy's disinflationary help fades on 13.8% piped-gas inflation. Bonds bid for labor weakness while SOFR still 6 basis points over funds says funding is not confirming the soft-landing cut story.
