# $70 Billion Bond Auction + An Absolute Melt-Up in Everything... Everything But the Dollar

## 1. Thesis

Into a year-end melt-up across stocks, gold, silver, and copper, a $70 billion 5-year auction cleared soft — a 0.1bp tail (the sixth in seven), weak 2.35x cover, record direct bidding absorbing what foreigners left — confirming the marginal Treasury buyer keeps shifting inward even as everything priced in dollars rips. The move is not shared prosperity but denominator failure: the S&P's 16.7% dollar gain is a 31% loss in gold and a mere 2.4% in euros, with the DXY breaking below its October lows and threatening its post-2008 uptrend. Silver's vertical run past $70 matters less for its level than for its plumbing — October's rare $2.61 spot-over-futures backwardation proved physical stress can precede price, and holiday-thin liquidity can rediscover it fast.
