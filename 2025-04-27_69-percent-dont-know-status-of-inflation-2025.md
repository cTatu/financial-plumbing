# 69% of Americans Don't Know This - The Status of Inflation in 2025

## 1. Thesis

With 69% telling CBS Trump is not doing enough to lower prices just as March printed outright month-over-month deflation, he made this video to reconcile lived price-level pain with rate-of-change and market reality: the cumulative index is up 22.7% since November 2020 and people feel that level, while breakevens, the 10-year yield, and core CPI have all fallen roughly 10-53bp since inauguration. The point is definitional — voters want a falling index that post-1980 history only delivers in recession — so the market is pricing less inflation since Liberation Day, not more.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] [09:25] The market rejects the tariff-inflation story because the 10-year breakeven has traded below its 2.33% pre-announcement level every day since Liberation Day.
- [TAKE] [11:08] The 10-year yield falling 53bp signals lower nominal-growth expectations, because a bond market worried about inflation would push yields higher not lower.
- [TAKE] [04:00] The economist-versus-public disconnect is level versus rate-of-change, since people feel the cumulative 22.7% rise in the index while economists cite the 1.8% yearly change.
- [TAKE] [05:50] Promising a lower price level without a recession misunderstands the index, because since 1980 prices only briefly fell in the 2008 recession and otherwise only disinflate.
- [TAKE] [12:31] Core CPI and the 10-year yield falling about 50bp together confirms a disinflationary trend since inauguration rather than tariff pass-through.
