# Breaking: French Debt Downgraded - 3rd Cut in Two Weeks Worsens Fiscal Crisis

## 1. Thesis

Scope's downgrade is France's third in two weeks and prices a doom loop the US cannot imagine at these yields: a near-6% twin-deficit burden on 0.8% growth with 7.5% unemployment, net interest marching from under 2% toward 3% of GDP by 2029, and a 237bp 30Y-2Y steepener that already charges OATs more than Greek bonds and 82bp over Bunds versus 25bp at Macron's election. With parliament too fragmented to pass the 2023-2027 consolidation path and the prime minister ruling out a 2026 austerity budget, France's only real buffer is long average maturity — the opposite of America's bill-heavy funding — while oil dependence and a shrinking tax base grind the revenue pie flat.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] Weak growth starves revenue because the tax slice needs the GDP pie to grow, and 1.1% growth guarantees repeated shortfalls.
- [TAKE] Parliamentary fragmentation makes the 2023-2027 consolidation path unimplementable, and the prime minister already rules out a 2026 austerity budget after a two-year delay.
- [TAKE] Long average maturity is France's only real buffer, the opposite of America's bill-heavy issuance, and losing it would push the double-B public-finance score toward junk.
- [TAKE] The 237bp bear steepener with 0.9% inflation proves the long end prices fiscal risk rather than growth, confirmed by OATs yielding more than Greek bonds.
- [TAKE] Twin deficits without reserve-currency privilege leave France no cushion, so shortfalls must be financed by paying ever-higher yields or running down foreign-exchange reserves.
