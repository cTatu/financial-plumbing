# The Fed Is Dead: Who Controls the Money Supply & Interest Rates?

## 1. Thesis

The Fed neither sets market rates nor prints most money: the 2-year Treasury leads the funds rate by months with 120-140bp of advance signal, and private banks create 92% of deposits with no reserve requirement since March 2020, so the funds range is a narrow overnight plumbing tool around a $3.3T reserve base inside a $22T M2 and $115T stock-plus-housing dollar system. That architecture explains the channel breakdown — M2 growing 5% a year and 6.5% on a 100-month average against 3.2% official inflation, with credit flowing into assets at 14% equity growth — which is why cutting into 3% inflation reads as fiscal dominance rather than stimulus.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] The bond market sets the funds path because trillions in 2-year trading price the overnight trajectory months before the Fed moves the range.
- [TAKE] The funds rate is plumbing control rather than economic control because it governs overnight dollar financing while households borrow against long yields and asset values.
- [TAKE] Private banks print the money stock because extending a loan creates the matching deposit, leaving the Fed's reserves as a small settlement base with no reserve-requirement brake.
- [TAKE] Credit creation favors assets over output because $115 trillion of equity and housing value absorbs newly created dollars through wealth, margin, and securities-backed lending rather than small-business loans.
- [TAKE] Official inflation understates monetary expansion because 6.5% average M2 growth against 3.2% CPI leaves the gap in asset prices and purchasing-power erosion.
