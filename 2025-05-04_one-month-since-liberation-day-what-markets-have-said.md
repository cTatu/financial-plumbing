# One Month Since 'Liberation Day' - What Markets Have Said

## 1. Thesis

He made this video to adjudicate the tariff story one month after Liberation Day by letting cross-asset prices vote: the nominal S&P recovery to pre-tariff levels is built on hope of deals that do not exist, while the dollar, oil, breakevens, and real yields tell a different story of disinflation plus fiscal risk. Stocks are flat, but the dollar is 3% weaker, oil is 17% lower, inflation expectations are lower, and the 10-year is higher only because the term premium rose 20bp. That split is framed as a regime signal — tariffs alone cannot fix twin deficits and an overvalued dollar, so the market is starting to price a weaker-dollar, Mar-a-Lago-style reordering against gold and scarce assets.
