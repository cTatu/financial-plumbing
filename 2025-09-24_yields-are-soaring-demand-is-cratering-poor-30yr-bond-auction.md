# Yields are Soaring, Demand is Cratering - Poor 30yr Bond Auction

## 1. Thesis

The UK's £1.5 billion 30-year gilt sale drew only £4.6 billion of bids — the weakest demand since 2022 — with a 1.4bp tail doubling last month's 0.8bp despite activist issuance suppressing supply and QT tilting away from the long end. With the 30-year at 5.5%, inflation at 3.8% against 1.2% growth, a deficit near 4% of GDP, and interest up ~25% year-over-year from £75 billion to £95 billion, tax-hike remedies are shrinking revenue as a share of GDP while millionaires flee — a textbook stagflationary debt doom loop in which each extra gilt lifts yields, interest, and deficits together.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] [03:45] Activist issuance plus QT restraint at the long end is failing in the UK because demand decay overwhelms every supply-management lever.
- [TAKE] [11:00] The UK runs textbook stagflation in which 3.8% inflation with 1.2% growth leaves no room to cut without negative real yields or to hike without deepening the loop.
- [TAKE] [08:45] Interest paid with newly issued bonds turns each yield rise into a larger deficit and more supply, which is the mechanical core of the doom loop.
- [TAKE] [12:15] Tax hikes worsen the arithmetic because slower growth plus capital flight shrinks revenue as a share of GDP for a third straight year.
- [TAKE] [15:52] The long end prices fiscal risk rather than growth, which is why gilt yields climb while growth falls — the inverse of a healthy expansion steepener.

## 4C Predicts

- [PREDICTS] [16:30] Bear steepening runs until yield-curve control or QE returns to force yields down. -> check: 30Y higher with QT continuing and no BoE buying; 30Y falling 50bp-plus on renewed BoE gilt buying refutes it.
- [PREDICTS] [11:35] The UK cannot run negative real yields with inflation at 3.8% without inflation accelerating toward 5% or 6%. -> check: CPI higher if the BoE cuts toward sub-inflation rates; CPI falling toward 2.5% alongside cuts refutes it.
