# PCE Inflation, Silent Depression + NVDA top?

## 1. Thesis

In-line PCE confirms theere is no disinflation to price: core PCE at 2.9% with core CPI at 3.1% and PPI above 3% keeps inflation near 3% while the Fed discusses cuts, and the 68% cumulative price rise since 2000 explains why 0.5%-a-month nominal spending with 1.5% real growth plus a 4.4% savings rate reads as a silent depression. The Q2 3.3% GDP print was an import-arithmetic bounce that the fresh $103B goods deficit already threatens to reverse, and NVIDIA's post-earnings spike above the prior high on double volume with an immediate dump is the textbook liquidity grab that often prints near-term tops.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] The hot Q2 GDP print borrowed from the future because collapsing imports flattered the arithmetic after tariff front-running bloated Q1 imports.
- [TAKE] Inflation has built a higher floor near 3% because core PCE at 2.9% with core CPI at 3.1% has set higher year-over-year lows for twelve months.
- [TAKE] Nominal spending misleads because a 0.5% monthly gain mostly reflects 3%-plus prices while inflation-adjusted spending grows only 1.5% and flatlined since December 2024.
- [TAKE] Twin deficits explain the savings collapse because a nation running fiscal plus current-account deficits can accumulate only debt on a net basis rather than savings.
- [TAKE] The NVIDIA wick-and-dump is a liquidity hunt because a large seller pushed price above the high to trigger automatic buy orders and then sold into that unlocked liquidity.
