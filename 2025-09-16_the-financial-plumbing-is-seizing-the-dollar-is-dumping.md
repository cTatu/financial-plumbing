# The Financial Plumbing is Seizing & the Dollar is Dumping

## 1. Thesis

Dollar funding seized on September 16 with SOFR at 4.51% above the 4.50% discount-window ceiling and 18 basis points over 4.33% fed funds — the worst non-calendar spike in five years — because the $850 billion TGA rebuild has $200 billion left with only $17 billion in the RRP and bank reserves down to $3.1 trillion and 14% of M2. Instead of the usual haven bid, DXY broke to its lowest weekly close since February 2022 below a 15-year trend, while SPX, Bitcoin, and VIX all registered the classic funding-stress signature on a day contaminated by tax deadlines ahead of the September 17 FOMC.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] The SOFR-minus-funds spread measures Fed control because free-market dollar cost above the administered rate means scarce dollars command a premium.
- [TAKE] Window-dressing cannot explain September 16 because the persistent September 5 onward spread sits outside month-end and quarter-end effects.
- [TAKE] The TGA must drain reserves because the $200 billion remainder cannot come from a $17 billion RRP, pushing reserves toward $2.9 trillion as M2 grows.
- [TAKE] Reserves-to-M2 near 13% to 14% warns of breakage because that zone preceded 2019 repo seizure and three of the four largest 2023 bank failures.
- [TAKE] Dollar weakness into a squeeze inverts the haven rule because scarce dollars normally lift DXY, so joint funding stress with selling marks reserve-bid erosion.

## 4C Predicts

- [PREDICTS] Continued SOFR pressure forces the Fed to abandon the TGA path and signal balance-sheet expansion at or after the September 17 press conference. -> check: SOFR holding wide with TGA capped or QT guidance shifting; SOFR collapsing with QT intact refutes it.
- [PREDICTS] SPX and Bitcoin draw down with VIX spiking while the spread stays wide, repeating the funding-stress pattern. -> check: equities and crypto lower with VIX higher into wider prints; risk higher with spread normalizing refutes it.
- [PREDICTS] DXY keeps breaking its 15-year line rather than haven-bidding on scarce dollars. -> check: DXY lower weekly closes with SOFR wide; DXY spiking with SOFR wide restores the haven rule and refutes it.
