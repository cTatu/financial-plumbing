# German Bond Yields are Soaring - Trouble for the US Bond Market?

## 1. Thesis

Germany's 30-year bund within 3bp of a 14-year high is a bear steepener built from two durable drivers — a defense-led fiscal expansion and an industrial revival — that together lift nominal growth and term premium at once. Because bunds sit 130-140bp below US 30-years on 2.2% versus 6% nominal growth, any further rise makes interest-bearing euros the haven alternative to Treasuries, repeating the April pattern where US rates rose while the dollar fell as European capital rotated home.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] The bund bear steepener reflects both higher nominal growth from defense-led industrial output and higher term premium from upside deficit surprises.
- [TAKE] Defense spending transmits powerfully in Germany because manufacturing is a top GDP component, unlike the services-heavy US where deficits stimulate less output.
- [TAKE] Rising bund yields invite rotation out of Treasuries because interest-bearing euros dominate plain euros once the dollar bull gives way to a euro bull.
- [TAKE] The April breakdown where US yields rose versus Europe while the dollar fell marks foreign selling of Treasuries followed by selling dollars for euros.
- [TAKE] Euro strength above 1.20 threatens the thesis because the ECB will resist appreciation that undercuts German exports and the €20 billion trade surplus.

## 7. Follow-ups

- Watch German 30Y against the 2011 high with Q4 supply: holding above with further upward deficit revisions confirms fiscal-plus-growth repricing, while a 20bp-plus fall on weak industry says recession risk returned.
- Watch US-Europe 10Y spread with EUR/USD near 1.20: spread wider with euro firmer confirms Treasury-to-bund rotation, while spread wider with dollar surging says the pre-April rate-differential regime is back.
- Watch German industrial production after its first positive print since May 2023: two more positive months with business expectations rising confirms the nominal-growth leg, while a relapse to contraction says stimulus has not transmitted.
