# Record Jobs Revisions - Is Deflation & Recession Coming?

## 1. Thesis

Yesterday's benchmark revision erased 911,000 payrolls and today's PPI printed minus 0.1% month over month, but neither proves deflation or imminent recession on its own: the revision is 0.6% of payrolls with a far lower 30,000-60,000 breakeven under restricted immigration, and the soft PPI sits inside 3.1% core CPI, 2.9% core PCE, and a commodity base building since late 2024. The bond market's bull flattening and SOFR still 7 basis points over funds price labor weakness, yet the 30-year up 80 basis points since cuts began plus gold and homes at highs argue the Fed cutting 25-50 basis points into 6% nominal growth risks re-accelerating the same services-led inflation it claims to have beaten.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] [05:17] Scale matters more than the headline because a 0.6% revision triples the 0.2% ten-year average without erasing the expansion.
- [TAKE] [08:11] Breakeven payrolls collapse under tight immigration because 30,000-60,000 now holds unemployment steady versus 150,000-180,000 during labor-supply surge.
- [TAKE] [06:38] One soft PPI month does not equal deflation because single-month negatives recur back to 2010 while annual averages carry the signal.
- [TAKE] [04:08] Commodities bottoming since October 2024 threatens the disinflation engine because energy and goods provided the drag now reversing to 2.7%.
- [TAKE] [11:28] Recession calls face a funding contradiction because an 800-900bp cyclical widening turns a 7% deficit into 15% of GDP in new issuance.
