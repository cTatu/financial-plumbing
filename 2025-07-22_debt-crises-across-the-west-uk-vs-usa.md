# Debt Crises Across the West- UK vs USA

## 1. Thesis

Britain is the cleaner warning light for the same Western debt spiral: a smaller deficit and longer 16-year gilt duration buy time, but gilt yields are bear-steepening like Treasuries, interest already eats 17% of revenue, and debt compounding has outrun GDP since 1997 while gold in pounds has nearly doubled. The US screens worse on nearly every flow and stock metric — 7% deficit versus 1%, 120-125% debt versus 95%, 20-21% interest share versus 17% — so the UK episode reads as the preview, with reserve-currency fade deciding how much longer America can pay more for longer.

## 2. Key Claims

- [00:09] UK national debt stands at nearly 2.9 trillion pounds, or almost 100% of GDP. (as of Jul 2025)
- [03:36] The UK current-account deficit is about 3.3% of GDP and the trade deficit is about 2.2% of GDP, with a 226-billion-pound goods deficit partly offset by a 194-billion-pound services surplus. (as of 2025)
- [05:40] UK government spending runs about 1.4 trillion pounds per year versus about 1.1 trillion pounds of revenue, leaving a 20-billion-pound monthly fiscal deficit. (as of Jul 2025)
- [06:22] The fiscal deficit is about 1% of GDP in the UK versus about 7% of GDP in the US. (as of Jul 2025)
- [06:54] Debt-to-GDP is about 95% in the UK versus about 120% to 125% in the US. (as of Jul 2025)
- [07:14] Government spending is about 38% of GDP with revenue at about 31% of GDP in the UK, versus about 23% to 24% spending share in the US. (as of Jul 2025)
- [08:30] Average UK gilt duration is about 16 years, or about double the US average, after issuing 40-year and 50-year gilts to term out debt. (as of Jul 2025)
- [11:00] UK interest costs run about 16.5 billion pounds per month, or about 196 billion pounds annualized, equal to about 17% of revenue versus 20% to 21% in the US. (as of Jul 2025)
- [13:54] The 30-year gilt yield jumped from about 2.4% to about 4.8% in about one week during the 2022 Liz Truss mini-budget episode. (as of 2022 episode)
- [15:39] UK government debt rose 756% versus GDP up 156% since 1997 with GDP per capita up only 39%, while interest expense grew 6 times faster than GDP. (as of 1997-Jul 2025)

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] [01:44] The debt death spiral runs from higher debt to higher inflation risk to higher yields to higher interest to higher deficits to more supply and back to higher yields.
- [TAKE] [08:58] The UK bought flexibility by terming out debt at low rates into 40-year and 50-year gilts while the US funded in short bills that must be constantly rolled.
- [TAKE] [10:43] Gilts show the same bear steepener as Treasuries since cuts began, with 10-year through 50-year yields rising while policy rates fall.
- [TAKE] [22:41] Debt crises arrive when borrowing cost exceeds nominal growth, which now binds in both countries as 10-year yields sit above 3.9% UK and 4.4% US growth.
- [TAKE] [29:57] Reserve status lets America run larger imbalances for longer, but a decade of falling dollar FX-reserve share and falling foreign-held debt share says the edge is fading.
