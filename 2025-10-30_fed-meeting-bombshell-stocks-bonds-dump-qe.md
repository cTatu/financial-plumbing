# Fed Meeting Bombshell: Stocks, Bonds Dump Lower - Is QE Returning?

## 1. Thesis

The Fed cut 25 basis points as expected but Powell's "not a foregone conclusion" language on December repriced future cuts, so stocks fell with VIX up while bond yields rose across the curve in a bear steepener. With inflation above target for 240 weeks and the four-year average running 2.40% above 2.00%, cutting into 3.00% inflation reads as fiscal dominance, while Powell's unusual admission of repo pressure plus the December-1 reinvestment of MBS principal into T-bills frames the meeting as the QT-to-reserve-growth pivot that is QE-ish in balance-sheet effect even if normal 3.00%-to-5.00% growth rather than 2020-scale QE.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] Cutting into 3.00% inflation after 240 weeks above target admits fiscal dominance because debt service needs lower rates while prices keep rising.
- [TAKE] Bonds sold off after the cut because the market had priced further easing, so Powell's December language forced yields to reprice higher across the curve.
- [TAKE] Powell discussing SOFR spreads, IORB pressure, and Standing Repo use matters because the Fed rarely comments on boring funding plumbing unless strain is serious.
- [TAKE] Reinvesting MBS runoff into bills stabilizes then grows the balance sheet with GDP because reserves and currency must expand to support nominal growth.
- [TAKE] Slow balance-sheet growth is QE-ish but not 2020 QE because 3.00%-to-5.00% annual debasement lifts hard assets without the 200.00% money-supply surge and 50.00% reversal.
