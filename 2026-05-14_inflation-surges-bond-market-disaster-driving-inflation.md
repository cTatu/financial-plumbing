# Inflation Surges, Bond Market Disaster: What's driving inflation, and where are we headed?

## 1. Thesis

Inflation is re-accelerating through energy rather than broad overheating: PPI has spiked to 6%, ISM prices-paid leads PPI by two months, and PPI leads CPI by two months, so the CPI pop is still early. With real wages negative, savings near 4%, revolving credit growing at 9% into rising delinquencies, and the 10-year up 50 basis points on higher breakevens plus term premium, importers face a balance-of-payments drain that shrinks the buyer base for $1.8 trillion of Treasury supply plus hyperscaler and refinancing waves — leaving recessionary demand destruction and eventual Fed monetization as the resolution.

- [TAKE] [01:36] The CPI pop is mainly energy and refined products flowing through oil, gasoline, diesel, jet fuel, plastics, aluminum, helium, and natural gas.
- [TAKE] [05:34] Negative real wages plus an already-low savings buffer forces dissaving and then higher-cost revolving debt into rising delinquencies.
- [TAKE] [10:10] The US curve shows a bear flattener with long-end yields driven by term premium over deficits and geopolitics while T-bills follow the Fed.
- [TAKE] [15:12] Importers moving from creditor to debtor means less foreign buying of Treasuries, hyperscaler debt, and the $1 trillion 2026 refinancing plus $1 trillion new corporate supply.
