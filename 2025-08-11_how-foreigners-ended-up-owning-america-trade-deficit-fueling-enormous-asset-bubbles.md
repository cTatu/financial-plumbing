# How Foreigners Ended up Owning America - How the trade deficit is fueling enormous asset bubbles

## 1. Thesis

The $1.8 trillion annualized trade deficit must be matched dollar-for-dollar by foreign buying of US assets, and that forced $1.8 trillion bid is the mechanical engine behind stock, housing, and top-1% wealth inflation. The deficit hit 6% of GDP in Q1 2025, a level seen only into the 2006 housing peak; with the Fed's balance sheet now shrinking, the capital-account surplus again explains asset prices, including $80 billion into US real estate in 2023 alone. The policy choice is therefore binary: keep the deficit and keep foreigners accumulating America, or balance trade and withdraw the bid.
