# Homes Sales at 2008 Lows - But Prices at All-time Highs

## 1. Thesis

Fresh sales prints confirm the broken-housing regime from two days earlier: volume sits at GFC levels while prices print new highs because thin transactions let a few wealthy cash buyers set the marginal comp for every home on the street. With mortgage-financed buyers sidelined, initial claims flat, and no forced sellers, the price-to-sales ratio has doubled and national prices can grind sideways-to-higher on 4M units — a correction requires a labor-break catalyst that reprices through volume first.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] [05:12] Transactions no longer predict price because the historical price-sales correlation broke after 2020 when cash buyers replaced mortgage buyers.
- [TAKE] [08:44] Low liquidity stretches price because one buyer paying the ask reprices every neighboring home the way one $6.00 options print reprices every contract.
- [TAKE] [08:59] Wealth concentration sustains the top because the top 1% alone could absorb the entire $3 trillion annual single-family flow for about 20 years.
- [TAKE] [16:41] Record affordability plus penny-stock mechanics describe the same condition where volume is irrelevant and only the marginal cash bid sets the mark.
- [TAKE] [20:04] Absent forced selling the national price holds because flat initial claims and 4.1%-area unemployment supply no catalyst that turns holders into sellers.
