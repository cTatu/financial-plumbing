# Dollar Gets Dumped and Bond Market Shows more Problems

## 1. Thesis

Thursday broke the safe-haven rulebook: the dollar crashed as much as 2.5% intraday toward critical 100-101 support while long-end yields jumped again, including a 13-basis-pointnrise in the 30-year on the same day as an A-plus auction. Cool CPI, a strong sale, and a 20% oil collapse should all have pushed yields down, so the joint stock-bond-dollar fall leaves foreign selling plus basis-trade unwind as the remaining explanation. The deeper warning is mechanical: every $100-billion-plus slice of trade-deficit reduction removes a matching slice of price-insensitive foreign demand just as Treasury supply accelerates, pointing toward bank-balance-sheet absorption or eventual Fed monetization.
