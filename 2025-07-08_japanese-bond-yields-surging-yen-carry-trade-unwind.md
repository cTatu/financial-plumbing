# Japanese Bond Yields Surging: Yen Carry Trade Unwind and Financial Crisis?

## 1. Thesis

The 30-basis-point weekly spike in long JGBs past 3% is normalization rather than implosion: after lost decades of 0% policy, negative rates, and yield-curve control, wages, inflation, bank lending, household spending, and a ¥3.4T current-account beat all point to an economy finally heating, so yields are repricing toward nominal growth with a 30Y-2Y bear-steepener from 70-80bp to 230bp. Fiscal fragility is real at 235-240% debt-to-GDP with consumption-tax-cut talk and a weak 5-year auction, but a yen-carry unwind is not underway because USD/JPY bottomed near 142 and reflects dollar strength since mid-April — the opposite of the July 2024 rapid yen spike that forced S&P liquidation. The watch variable is the Liberation Day divergence where US-over-Japan spreads rose while the dollar fell, which would turn to repatriation pressure on Treasuries, dollars, and US equities if it resumes.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] Bond yields should approximate nominal growth, so rising JGBs with wages and activity heating read as normalization rather than automatic meltdown.
- [TAKE] Japan's persistent current-account surplus means savings accumulate at home, so US-Japan debt-to-GDP comparisons without balance-of-payments context mislead.
- [TAKE] The July 2024 unwind required rapid yen strength that put yen-funded dollar positions underwater, while current dollar strength since mid-April is the opposite setup.
- [TAKE] The post-Liberation Day pattern of higher US-over-Japan spreads with a weaker dollar signals foreign selling of Treasuries and dollars for yen.
- [TAKE] Japan faces a choice between letting yields clear with domestic savings absorbing duration or resuming QE/YCC and letting the yen take the adjustment.

## 4C Predicts

- [PREDICTS] Yen weakness returning toward year-to-date highs while 30Y and 40Y JGBs make new highs marks disorderly stress. -> check: USD/JPY with long JGB yields over coming weeks; yen firmness with yields elevated refutes it.
- [PREDICTS] Higher JGB yields pull Japanese savings home out of Treasuries and US equities while US-Japan spreads sit flat to US-favoring. -> check: yen strength plus TIC Japanese net selling over coming months; renewed dollar strength with foreign buying refutes it.
- [PREDICTS] The US-Japan spread-versus-USD/JPY correlation stays broken after Liberation Day if repatriation continues. -> check: US10Y-JP10Y spread rising with USD/JPY falling; recoupling with dollar rising on wider spreads refutes it.
