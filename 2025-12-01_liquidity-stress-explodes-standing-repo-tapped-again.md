# Liquidity Stress Explodes - Standing Repo Tapped AGAIN, Global Bond Market Sell-Off

## 1. Thesis

The fourth straight heavy Standing Repo draw collides with a global duration selloff: SOFR held 12bp over the 4.00% ceiling for a third day while the 75th percentile sat about 20bp over, meaning roughly $800B funds dearer than the backstop, even as Japanese 2s and 10s spiked on renewed BOJ-hike bets. The presenter reads this as a two-sided dollar squeeze — TGA-funded reserve drainage at home plus yen-carry repatriation abroad — where selling Treasuries to buy yen and JGBs pushes US yields up, the dollar down, and equities and Bitcoin lower while silver breaks to new highs.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] A third over-ceiling median with $25.00 billion drawn on a non-quarter-end December 1 proves scarcity rather than month-end window-dressing.
- [TAKE] The $500.00 billion facility cap cannot cover the $800.00 billion over-bound cohort, so stigma plus haircuts plus sheer size keep market rates above the backstop.
- [TAKE] Japanese 2-year strength signals BOJ-hike repricing that pulls capital toward yen, which forces Treasury sales, dollar sales, and upward US yield pressure.
- [TAKE] Falling bank reserves with a $600.00 billion TGA rebuild explain widening SOFR spreads because reserves and spreads move in tight inverse correlation.
- [TAKE] Silver breaking out while a triple-redundant CME cites cooling reads as blockade or short-cover stress rather than plumbing coincidence.
