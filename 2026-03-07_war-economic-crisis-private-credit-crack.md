# WAR: An Economic Crisis Blooms as Private Credit Starts to Crack

## 1. Thesis

A full-blown Middle East war has effectively closed the Strait of Hormuz five days in, converting energy into a simultaneous inflation tax and current-account shock while equities were already pricing AI disruption. The market rotation since late October, software collapse, record private-credit defaults, redemption gates, and bank exposures show credit stress predated the war, and the oil spike plus bear steepener and VIX surge now threaten to turn that crack into a recessionary crisis constrained less by dollars than by US industrial capacity.
