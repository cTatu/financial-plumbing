# Pt 2: The 'Silent Depression'- How the Economy was Changed Forever

## 1. Thesis

After 2008 private-bank credit creation stopped outpacing M2, and three substitutes filled the void — federal debt doubling from 63% to 121% of GDP on a 6% deficit that only recessions used to justify, corporate profits growing faster than GDP through labor arbitrage, and financial assets with the S&P 500-to-GDP Buffett gauge hitting 200% — so GDP, receipts and top-10% wealth look supported while log-trend real GDP sits $9 trillion light at $23 trillion versus $32 trillion, personal income sits $10 trillion light at $25 trillion versus $35 trillion or $58,000 per worker per year, and velocity, real income-to-M2 and SPX-to-gold all trace the same post-2000 break that inverted curves and post-2008 regulation made permanent.

- [TAKE] [01:49] Banks borrow at short durations and lend at long durations, so the 1-year-to-10-year spread near 3.00% was their profit engine before inversion.

- [TAKE] [06:01] Post-2008 regulation plus inversion pushed private credit below M2 growth, and government debt, corporate-profit share and SPX-to-GDP grew faster than GDP to fill the gap.
