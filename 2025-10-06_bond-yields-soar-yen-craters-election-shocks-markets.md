# Breaking: Bond Yields Soar, Yen Craters as Election Shocks Markets

## 1. Thesis

Japan's surprise election of an Abe-disciple prime minister repriced Abenomics in one session: the yen crashed, the 30-year JGB yield printed a record high while the short end priced out Bank of Japan hikes, and the Nikkei ripped 5% on the same easy-money impulse. The read is that renewed monetary-plus-fiscal expansion first turbocharges the yen carry trade — borrowed yen buying dollars, Treasuries, and equities — which can cap US yields and lift US risk, unless JGB yields rise far enough to repatriate Japanese capital home and push US yields higher instead. With Japan joining a global run-it-hot turn at 2.7% inflation, US markets inherit both the tailwind and the repatriation risk.
