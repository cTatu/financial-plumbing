# Historic Intraday Volatility - Monday Apr 7th Markets Recap

## 1. Thesis

Monday's fake-news 90-day-pause headline exposed how fragile positioning has become, whipping the S&P through its largest intraday swing on record before fading it entirely. The louder signal was not equities but duration: the entire curve sold off 30-plus basis points from the lows into what should have been a flight to safety, steepening 2s10s to May-2022 levels. With the dollar briefly selling alongside stocks and bonds overnight and haven demand flowing to the franc, yen, and gold instead, the tape reads as early capital rotation out of dollar assets funded by years of twin deficits — the balance-sheet stress that makes an emerging-market-style selloff possible.
