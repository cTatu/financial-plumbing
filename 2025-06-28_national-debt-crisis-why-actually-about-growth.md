# The National Debt Crisis - Why It's Actually About Growth

## 1. Thesis

The debt crisis is not the $36-37 trillion level but the growth rate of debt versus the growth rate of GDP that services it: each successive regime — post-1950, Great Society, 1971, early-1980s trade deficits, 2001 China shock — widens the gap until $1.00 of new debt buys only $0.35-$0.50 of output while interest outruns GDP 4-to-1, and with the 10-year yield crossing above nominal growth after breaking a 50-year downtrend, larger government share plus higher debt-to-GDP mechanically drags future growth and forces bigger deficits to stand still.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] [03:24] Productive debt is judged by income relative to borrowing, so GDP growth versus debt growth matters more than the $36-37T level.
- [TAKE] [05:12] Rising debt-to-GDP crowds out private productive investment and lowers the GDP return per dollar of new borrowing.
- [TAKE] [10:36] Persistent trade and current-account deficits must be funded by a capital-account surplus, so foreigners recycle dollars into Treasuries, stocks, corporate bonds, and real estate.
- [TAKE] [10:52] Reserve-currency status creates Triffin's dilemma where America must run ever-larger trade deficits that hollow out Rust Belt industry while bidding up financial assets by ~$150B per month.
- [TAKE] [21:24] When the 10-year yield exceeds nominal GDP growth, debt-to-GDP rises mechanically and forces larger deficits that further depress growth and lift rates.

## 7. Follow-ups

- Watch 10-year yield minus nominal GDP growth: persistently positive with debt-to-GDP rising confirms the crossover thesis; renewed negative gap with falling debt-to-GDP refutes it.
- Watch deficit-to-GDP with 10-year and 30-year yields: 6%-7% deficits with higher long yields confirms rates pricing fiscal risk; yields falling to new lows on unchanged deficits refutes it.
- Watch debt-versus-GDP increments: each $1T of new debt adding ~$350B-$500B or less of GDP confirms diminishing returns; $1T adding near $1T of GDP argues productivity returned.
