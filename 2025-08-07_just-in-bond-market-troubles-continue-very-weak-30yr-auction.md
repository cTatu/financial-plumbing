# JUST IN: Bond Market Troubles Continue - Very Weak 30yr Auction

## 1. Thesis

The $25 billion 30-year auction tails 2.1bp with bid-to-cover at 2.27, the weakest since September 2023, confirming the prior day's $42 billion 10-year weakness was not a one-off. Even after shrinking long-end supply, indirect demand keeps falling while primary dealers take 17%, the most in 12 months — so duration, not size, is the problem, and the market prices it as a bear steepener where all yields rise but 30-year values fall fastest on fiscal-dominance and Fed-independence fears.
