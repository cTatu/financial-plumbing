# Breaking: CPI Inflation Report Just Out — Here's Why It Could Get Worse

## 1. Thesis

December CPI printed 2.7% headline in line with Wall Street and 2.6% core slightly below, but the composition is the warning: about 90% of inflation is coming from services with every component contributing positively, while the energy drag that held the index down is flipping to a tailwind as oil breaks its downtrend. With shelter up 3.2%, electricity up 7.0%, piped gas up 10.8%, and inflation above the 2% target for 58 straight months, a commodity breakout from a ~3% floor — not a ~1.5% floor — would push CPI toward 3.5-4% quickly, even as the Fed keeps cutting and the 10-year spread over Fed funds sits at its widest since 2022.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] Everyday inflation is felt as the cumulative loss of purchasing power, not the rate of change, which is why a 37% decade rise explains sentiment better than a 2.7% print.
- [TAKE] Services dominance with all components positive means goods-tariff stories miss the driver, because shelter, food away, medical, and recreation are doing the work.
- [TAKE] Energy flipping from drag to contributor removes the one suppressant that kept CPI below 3.5%, so an oil breakout transmits almost directly to headline.
- [TAKE] The Fed cutting with inflation 100bp above target for nearly five years is fiscal-dominance behavior, not inflation-targeting behavior.
- [TAKE] Gold rising on the print while the 10-year ignores Fed funds confirms the denominator trade, because the long end prices credit while the Fed prices overnight money.
