# Stocks Tumble (again) As AI Jitters Grip Market + A 20yr Bond Auction: How's Demand for Long Bonds?

## 1. Thesis

A soft $13 billion 20-year auction still stopped through because equity weakness created a flight-to-duration bid, not because long-end fundamentals improved. The damage concentrates in the same narrow AI winners that carried the cap-weighted index — Nvidia, Taiwan Semi, Micron, Oracle, Broadcom down 7-20% in seven days — while equal-weight S&P and Russell breadth has outperformed since early-to-mid November. With the 30-year up from 3.93% to 4.80% since the Fed started cutting and the 30s-2s spread 35bp steeper since October, the market prices supply and term premium at the long end while the 2-year alone prices cuts.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] The stop-through signals rotation rather than health because equity-led weakness pushed buyers into duration even in a wonky, illiquid 20-year tenor.
- [TAKE] Dealer take matters because primary dealers absorbed 12.5% versus an 11.4% average, leaving a larger buyer-of-last-resort footprint beneath the headline strength.
- [TAKE] Concentration is the core equity risk because a handful of Mag-7 and chip names drove the cap-weighted index higher and now drag it lower while breadth improves underneath.
- [TAKE] Oracle credit-default swaps reflect rising perceived credit risk because protection pricing has traded up since September-October alongside the AI-CapEx debt build.
- [TAKE] The long end trades fiscal supply and inflation expectations while the 2-year trades Fed cuts, which is why 2-year yields fall as 30-year yields push toward 4.85-4.90%.
