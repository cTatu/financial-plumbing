# The Multi-Trillion Dollar 'Yen Carry Trade' Unwind - What is it & is it Here?

## 1. Thesis

The yen carry trade is borrowing free yen to buy dollars and US assets for two-way positive carry, funded by Japan's 5%-of-GDP current-account surplus recycling into Treasuries, equities, and real estate; the unwind test is yen strength with falling JGB yields as shorts cover, last seen in summer 2024. That test currently fails despite S&P pressure and multi-decade JGB yield highs because the dollar keeps strengthening on a dovish Takaichi outlook while the JGB 10-year yield rose instead of falling, so repatriation where higher JGBs pull savings home remains the risk rather than the present event.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] Free yen funding plus persistent yen weakness creates positive carry because borrowers profit both from dollar strength and rising US assets.
- [TAKE] Japan's trade surplus must accumulate as foreign assets because unconsumed national savings flow abroad by balance-of-payments arithmetic.
- [TAKE] America's trillion-dollar deficit must be matched by a capital-account surplus because foreign inflows fund US financial assets by accounting identity.
- [TAKE] True unwind requires yen strength plus JGB yields falling on haven buying because shorts buy yen to cover while US assets liquidate.
- [TAKE] Current dollar strength with JGB yields at multi-decade highs is the opposite setup because repatriation would instead lift yen and JGBs while pressing Treasuries and dollars.
