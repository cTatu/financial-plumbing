# Major Turning Point: Fed Cuts Rates, Ignores Inflation - Emerging Market Fiscal Dominance

## 1. Thesis

The September 17 cut of 25 basis points with core CPI at 3.1% and every inflation gauge nearer 3% than 2% abandons price stability for deficit finance: the median dot guides two more cuts into 7%-of-GDP procyclical borrowing while 1-month to 6-month bills price below the funds rate because Treasury must roll short. Retail sales print 5% nominal and 2.1% real on top-decile spending, the average continues to lose through 3% compounding, and gold pricing vertical gains already discounts negative real rates, a weaker dollar, and a K-shaped economy that rate cuts will widen rather than heal.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] A cut at 3% inflation with the SEP guiding more easing subordinates the mandate to deficit funding because short-rollover math cannot survive higher-for-longer rates.
- [TAKE] Headline inflation misreads tariff incidence because only 27 of 270 basis points comes from goods, so services stickiness rather than duties drives the overshoot.
- [TAKE] Labor softness reflects normalization because post-2020 monetary and 10-to-20-million immigration swings unwind slowly without requiring a recession call.
- [TAKE] Record assets signal denominator failure because gold, equities, and homes rise together only when each dollar purchases less at an accelerating pace.
- [TAKE] Bill-heavy finance traps the Fed because rolling 1-month to 6-month paper bars hikes once inflation reaches 4%, so cuts must continue into price pressure.
