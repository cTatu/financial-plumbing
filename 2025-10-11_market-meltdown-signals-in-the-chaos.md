# Market Meltdown - Signals in the Chaos

## 1. Thesis

A rambling Trump threat to raise China tariffs by 100% into a functional embargo over rare-earth export controls vaporized about $1.8 trillion of S&P value in one session, with Nasdaq down 3.5% and megacap names off about 5% while front-month S&P futures fell 3.3% in a 2.6-sigma move seen only 19 times in a decade. The market read it as a negative growth shock because crude, copper, and the 10-year yield fell in unison, while bonds worldwide caught a haven bid unlike April's joint stock-bond-dollar exit. The regime signal was where safety did not go: the dollar sold off against the euro, yen, and Swiss franc on a risk-off day while gold closed up 1.3% with a bullish candle, and crypto printed the largest liquidation in history as crowded short-volatility and leveraged longs unwound together.
