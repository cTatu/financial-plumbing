# Payrolls Data Much Weaker than Expected - Labor Market Cracking?

## 1. Thesis

July payrolls added just 73,000 jobs against 110,000 expected while May and June were cut by a combined 258,000, leaving two months bordering negative prints and repricing Fed cuts through a one-day bull steepening. The internals confirm cracking beneath a 4.2% headline: U6 runs nearly double, 4.7 million work part-time for economic reasons, 1.8 million are long-term unemployed, new entrants surged by 275,000 to 985,000, and youth unemployment sits at 15%. Official wage gains still flatter reality once deflated by 8% annual M2 growth, and the larger framework since cuts began remains a bear steepener.
