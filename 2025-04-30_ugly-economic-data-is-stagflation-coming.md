# Ugly Economic Data - Is Stagflation Coming?

## 1. Thesis

With Q1 GDP printing negative alongside hot PCE and a Michigan expectations spike, he made this video to argue the stagflation scare dissolves once components replace headlines: the contraction is tariff front-running through record goods imports plus a marginal government drag, while March PCE was flat on the month and the disinflationary trend holds. The market's split reaction — 2-year bid on cut odds versus 10-year and 30-year selling off — plus steady TBAC supply guidance is framed as fiscal-premium steepening to watch, not 1970s-style stagflation confirmed.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] [07:54] Front-running of tariffs through record goods imports explains the GDP drag because the import-minus-export gap sits near historic highs since WWII rather than domestic demand collapsing.
- [TAKE] [10:52] Government spending subtracted from GDP, but about $150B of DOGE cuts on a $7T budget is a roughly 2% marginal tightening rather than austerity.
- [TAKE] [17:23] PCE details undercut the stagflation label because flat March month-over-month and 2.6% year-over-year extend the post-2022 disinflationary trend despite the times-four quarterly optics.
- [TAKE] [26:30] Michigan 5% expectations carry no money or expertise against a 2.25% breakeven and 4.16% nominal yield, so the survey-market gap is noise versus priced risk.
- [TAKE] [34:00] The curve reaction of a lower 2-year with a higher 10-year and 30-year is bear steepening on weak data, which signals duration and fiscal fear that steady TBAC supply news only briefly calmed.

## 7. Follow-ups

- Watch next GDP revision with goods trade: imports normalizing after front-running plus government neutral-to-positive confirms a one-off drag, while another negative print with steady imports says demand itself rolled.
- Watch PCE month-over-month with breakeven near 2.25%: another 0-0.1% PCE plus breakeven holding below 2.34% confirms disinflation, while breakeven jumping 10bp+ reframes the 3.5% annualized QoQ as pass-through.
- Watch 2-year near 3.66% against 10-year and 30-year highs: 2-year falling with long end holding up 9-11bp confirms bear steepening and fiscal premium, while long end reclaiming pre-print lows says TBAC steadiness won.
