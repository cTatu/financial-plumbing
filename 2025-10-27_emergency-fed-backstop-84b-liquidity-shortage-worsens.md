# Emergency Fed Backstop Hit AGAIN - Liquidity Shortage Worsens

## 1. Thesis

The Standing Repo Facility was hit for $8.4 billion on October 27 with increasing frequency, while reverse-repo minus standing-repo fell to negative $6 billion for the first time since July 2020 and SOFR sat at 4.24%, only 1bp under the discount-window ceiling. With no month-end, quarter-end, or tax-deadline excuse, the read is a regime shift from abundant to scarce dollars — everyone holds Treasury, agency, and MBS collateral but no cash — and the only durable fix is Fed reserve printing via Treasury and MBS purchases, which would likely make any 2019-style dislocation sharp but very short-lived.
