# Repo Soars to Record $20B, Bank Reserves Plummet to 5yr Low, SOFR Show Fed is Losing Control

## 1. Thesis

The morning Standing Repo Facility drew a $20.35 billion record since becoming standing, concentrated in mortgage-backed collateral, while SOFR sat 30bp over reverse-repo and 4bp over the 4.00% ceiling for a fourth straight day with the 99th percentile at a record. With bank reserves down to $2.8 trillion toward an estimated $2.7 trillion lowest-comfortable level and reserves-to-M2 back at the 13% line that preceded the January-2023 regional-bank failures, the tape shows price and quantity tightening together for three months, which is why the presenter argues only QE-scale reserve buying, not 25-50bp of cuts, can fix a counterparty-driven cash shortage now correlated with fresh regional-bank weakness.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] Record SRF use plus SOFR above the ceiling means price and quantity confirm the same shortage because emergency borrowing is rising as market clearing rates exceed the administered cap.
- [TAKE] Mostly mortgage-backed SRF collateral matters because banks are pledging less-liquid securities for cash, which fits a cash-shortage story rather than routine funding.
- [TAKE] Rate cuts cannot fix this scarcity because the problem is lendable reserves and counterparty willingness, while only asset purchases inject fresh dollars against bank securities.
- [TAKE] The 13%-of-M2 breakdown matters because the same line preceded the January-2023 regional failures, so coinciding Zions and Western Alliance weakness revives that warning.
- [TAKE] Inverted reserves tracking the SOFR-RRP average almost one-for-one means falling reserves and wider spreads are the same tightening phenomenon, echoing the QT-driven path into 2019.

## 4C Predicts

- [PREDICTS] The Fed is forced to restart balance-sheet expansion because QT plus bill supply keeps draining reserves into thin buffers. -> check: QT-end plus reserve-management buying confirms it; continued runoff with spreads normalizing refutes necessity.
- [PREDICTS] Liquidity-sensitive risk rebounds in a V-shape once the Fed injects reserves. -> check: SPX up with VIX down as SOFR compresses confirms it; continued equity weakness with spreads wide says stress still binds.
- [PREDICTS] Regional-bank stress tracks SOFR strain rather than diverging. -> check: Zions and Western Alliance falling further with spreads wide confirms linkage; banks recovering with spreads wide weakens the causal read.
