# Trump's War on Powell: Will Shadow Fed Crash Markets? - Weekly Recap

## 1. Thesis

Strong $39B 10-year and $22B 30-year stops mask a weaker trend because Treasury cut long-end supply while foreign share rolls over, and the market's real signal is a bear steepener with longs rising since the September 2024 cuts.
