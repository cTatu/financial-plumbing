# 10x In Gold Price? - Is SOFR Liquidity Stress Leading to Soaring Gold Prices?

## 1. Thesis

Gold's melt-up is a plumbing signal, not just an inflation trade: with the RRP buffer empty and every TGA-refill bill soaking liquidity, the SOFR-minus-RRP spread's rise since early 2024 maps almost tick-for-tick onto gold's $2,000 breakout, because scarce, balance-sheet-constrained dollars push sovereigns into the one bearer asset with no counterparty or seizure risk. Reframed against history the move is early rather than exhausted — restoring the 1910-1940 average 22% debt backing ($8.2T) or Roosevelt's 40% M2 backing ($8.8T) implies roughly $28,000-$30,000 per ounce, a 6-7x dollar devaluation that is still a fraction of gold's 2,182% run from 1965 to 1980.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] The SOFR-minus-RRP spread rising from early 2024 into gold's $2,000 breakout points to plumbing stress rather than headline inflation as the marginal driver of the bid.
- [TAKE] Gold functions as outside collateral because it carries no counterparty, seizure, or clearing risk, so sovereigns prefer it once the RRP buffer is empty and each TGA-refill bill soaks up liquidity.
- [TAKE] The $28,000-$30,000 per ounce arithmetic reframes the rally as partial dollar devaluation, and the 1965-1980 precedent of +2,182% shows a 6-7x move is historically precedented rather than inconceivable.
- [TAKE] Bitcoin decoupling from rising global M2 while gold surges reflects balance-sheet-constrained, sovereign-channeled liquidity rather than a broad risk-on bid.
