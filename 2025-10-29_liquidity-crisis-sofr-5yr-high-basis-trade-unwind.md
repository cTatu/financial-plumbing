# Liquidity Crisis as SOFR Spikes to 5yr High - Next Repo Crisis & Basis Trade Unwind

## 1. Thesis

Overnight stress hit a five-year extreme with SOFR 31bp over the reverse-repo rate and 4.31% — 6bp above the discount-window ceiling — on a non-quarter-end, non-month-end, non-tax day, alongside a record $10.20 billion non-quarter-end Standing Repo draw. With bank reserves down from $3.40 trillion to $2.90 trillion, the $600.00 billion TGA rebuild funded from reserves because the reverse-repo buffer is empty, and $110.00 billion-scale bill supply draining cash, the presenter moves the signal from yellow to red and frames today's FOMC as an end-of-QT decision with QE-scale reserve buying as the only durable fix before repo strain unwinds the levered cash-futures basis trade.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] SOFR above the ceiling plus a record non-quarter-end Standing Repo draw confirms red-light stress because price and quantity legs agree outside any calendar window.
- [TAKE] QT plus TGA rebuild plus $110.00 billion-scale bill supply drains reserves because Treasury cash-raising removes dollars while QT destroys them with no reverse-repo buffer left.
- [TAKE] Counterparty fear tightens as much as quantity because cash withheld from repo lifts SOFR even when dollars exist somewhere in the system.
- [TAKE] A trillion-scale basis trade must be financed in repo, so SOFR dislocations directly raise its carry cost and forced exits sell cash Treasuries while buying back futures.
- [TAKE] Ending QT only stops the drain while QE rebuilds reserves because only reserve-printing asset purchases inject new lendable cash against bank securities.

## 4C Predicts

- [PREDICTS] The Fed ends QT at today's meeting with language toward reserve growth or QE. -> check: statement ends runoff or guides bill-buying reserve adds; unchanged runoff pace refutes it.
- [PREDICTS] Persistent SOFR dislocation unwinds the basis trade with cash yields spiking versus futures. -> check: cash-futures basis widening toward the March-2020 pattern confirms it; stable basis with SOFR normalizing refutes transmission.
- [PREDICTS] Without QE-scale buying, stress escalates toward a 2019-style spike rather than fading. -> check: SRF taps climbing with spreads wide confirms it; spreads collapsing with no action refutes necessity.
