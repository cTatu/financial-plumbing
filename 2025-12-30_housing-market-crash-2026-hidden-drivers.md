# Housing Market CRASH in 2026? - The Hidden Drivers You Won't Hear About Anywhere Else

## 1. Thesis

The crash call misses the monetary plumbing: existing sales sit at 4.1 million near 2008 lows while nominal prices hold because cash buyers, a steepening yield curve reviving bank mortgage creation, falling mortgage rates from 2022 highs, 5% wage growth beating 1% home-price growth, and persistent foreign recycling of the trade deficit into US housing all support prices. Gold pricing near record affordability and the 50-year bond-bull breakout frame the real risk as financial repression ahead — negative real rates plus renewed Fed MBS buying and demand subsidies like a 50-year mortgage would re-accelerate nominal prices and worsen affordability rather than crash them, except in overbuilt Sunbelt pockets.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] Homes priced in gold or the S&P 500 sit near record affordability because monetary metals and equities outran house prices over decades.
- [TAKE] The first-ever divergence of the price index up and average mortgage size down proves cash and top-1% buyers carry the market while mortgaged buyers retreat.
- [TAKE] Bank mortgage creation drives home prices because a steeper curve pays banks to print new dollars as loans, so more lending means more bidding power for houses.
- [TAKE] Rate suppression is the transmission because Fed balance-sheet expansion lowers MOVE volatility and compresses the 2-point mortgage-over-Treasury spread.
