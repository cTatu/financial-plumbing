# Dollar Liquidity Shock Incoming? - Reverse Repo Hits Lowest Level in 4yrs

## 1. Thesis

The reverse-repo buffer is effectively gone at $28 billion versus a $2.5 trillion peak, so the Treasury's plan to rebuild the TGA from $464 billion to $850 billion must drain about $400 billion from bank reserves rather than from sterilized cash. That double drain is dollar-positive in price but liquidity-negative in quantity: fewer dollars lift the DXY in the near term while SOFR spikes above fed funds and a rising DXY/SPX ratio warn of pullbacks in stocks and gold. The tail risk is a 2019-style repo seizure when scarcity overwhelms willing lenders.
