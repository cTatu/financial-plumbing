# Liquidity Crisis Worsens - SRF Tapped, SOFR Spreads Blow Out to 2020 Highs

## 1. Thesis

A mid-October funding break outside any calendar window marks a regime flip from abundant to scarce dollars: an $8.35B Standing Repo Facility draw with SOFR above the discount-window ceiling, the SOFR-RRP spread widest since March 2020, and RRP-minus-SRF negative for the first time since 2020. The driver is structural rather than seasonal — a ~7%-of-GDP deficit requiring ~$2T of annual absorption, ongoing QT with reserves near $3T, and a ~$0.5T TGA rebuild with no RRP buffer left — replaying the 2019 QT-into-stress setup where only reserve creation fixed the plumbing, so the policy put is balance-sheet expansion rather than rate cuts.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] QT plus deficits plus the TGA rebuild plus an empty RRP all drain dollar liquidity because every new Treasury must be bought with dollars while QT destroys reserves.
- [TAKE] The SOFR-minus-RRP spread measures free-market dollar scarcity against the Fed's floor because repo cost above the administered rate means dollars command a premium.
- [TAKE] SOFR printing above the discount-window ceiling signals the Fed losing control of the overnight price of money because the market clears above the stated backstop.
- [TAKE] Rate cuts cannot fix repo stress because an overnight reserve shortage is a balance-sheet quantity problem, so ending QT and rebuilding reserves is the coherent tool.
- [TAKE] The 2019 response of printing reserves to buy Treasuries and MBS is the template because it injects new dollar liquidity directly into banks, foreshadowing QE in plumbing if stress deepens.
