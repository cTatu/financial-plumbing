# Major Stock Sell-Off： HUGE -3% Intraday Reversal Sends Stocks Market Sharply Lower

## 1. Thesis

A ~3% intraday reversal — the largest since April's Liberation Day chaos — broke the S&P 500 cleanly through its 100-day moving average around 6,610 on elevated volume despite a headline-strong payrolls print that first lifted futures. The presenter rejects payrolls as the cause because the S&P 5-minute top coincided with the USD/JPY top near 158, and instead attributes the drop to preemptive profit-taking by yen-carry holders facing trillions of exposure into likely BOJ intervention, producing systematic selling where implied correlation and VIX rise together rather than single-stock weakness.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] Payrolls do not explain the 10:40 AM reversal because futures rallied on the print and the S&P 5-minute top aligned with the USD/JPY top with no matching news wire.
- [TAKE] A ripping USD/JPY invites preemptive carry-trade profit-taking because holders with trillions of exposure prefer to sell US tech, S&P and Treasuries before BOJ buying of yen pushes dollar-yen down.
- [TAKE] The carry trade spans tech stocks, the S&P 500 and Treasuries, so de-grossing shows up as systematic selling where implied correlation rises with VIX and everything is sold for VaR reduction.
- [TAKE] Capitulation bottoms arrive with vertical VIX and implied-correlation spikes toward 35-40 plus a volume surge, which is why April's spike marked the low and today's incomplete move argues against an all-clear.
- [TAKE] Bitcoin leads risk in both directions after selling off for one to two months, so a Bitcoin low likely precedes the tradable S&P low.
