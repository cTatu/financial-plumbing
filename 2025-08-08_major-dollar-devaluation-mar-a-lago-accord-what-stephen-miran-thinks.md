# Major Dollar Devaluation, Mar-a-lago Accord, - What Stephen Miran Thinks

## 1. Thesis

Trump nominating CEA chair Stephen Miran to the Fed is read as the bridge from tariffs-first to currency-second: the dollar is 90.1% overvalued on IMF purchasing-power parity, more stretched than at the 1985 Plaza peak, forcing a $150 billion-per-month capital-account surplus that hollows manufacturing and bids financial assets. Miran's user's guide argues tariffs alone cannot balance trade because reserve demand keeps the dollar bid, so the next phase is a Mar-a-Lago-style multilateral or unilateral devaluation plus terming-out and user-fee tools that weaken the dollar back to fair value at the cost of lower dollar-asset prices and definancialization.

## 2. Key Claims

- [01:11] IMF purchasing-power parity puts dollar overvaluation at 90.1% in 2024 versus a 56.2% long-run average. (as of 2024 IMF print)
- [01:24] The DXY in the low 100s in 2023 was more overvalued than the 161 DXY peak into the 1985 Plaza Accord. (as of 2023 IMF print)
- [04:02] The current-account deficit runs about $150 billion per month or about $450 billion per quarter. (as of mid-2025 BEA)
- [02:28] Secondary-income outflows run about negative $100 billion per year on the current account. (as of mid-2025 BEA)
- [04:46] The capital-account surplus must run about $450 billion per quarter to mirror the current-account deficit. (as of mid-2025 BEA)
- [05:47] The top 10% of earners own 93% of US equities on a household basis. (as of 2025 Fed data as cited)
- [10:47] The US net international investment position sits at negative 95% of GDP in foreign net claims. (as of mid-2025 BEA)
- [13:53] US manufacturing has lost 5 million good-paying jobs to persistent overvaluation since the WTO era. (as of 2025 BLS as cited)
- [15:43] First-term 10% tariffs were offset by an almost 10% dollar appreciation versus the yuan with no US price lift. (as of 2018-2019 episode)
- [16:32] A decade of China tariff revenue equals roughly one-third of the cost of the tax cuts. (as of 2025 CBO arithmetic as cited)

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] [02:43] Reserve-asset demand forces foreigners to buy dollars at any price, so euro selling for Treasuries lifts the dollar and widens the trade deficit.
- [TAKE] [14:10] The capital-account surplus came first as the egg to the trade-deficit chicken because foreign Treasury buying forced America to run the deficit.
- [TAKE] [15:49] Currency offset let China pay the first-term tariff through a weaker yuan while Americans saw little inflation and the Treasury collected revenue.
- [TAKE] [18:19] Gradual monthly tariff escalation would have limited volatility, so April chaos reflects ignoring Miran's gradualism for sharp sudden moves.
- [TAKE] [21:26] Cutting the capital-account surplus must shrink the $150 billion-per-month bid into Treasuries, stocks, and real estate and trigger repatriation.
