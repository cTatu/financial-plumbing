# Something in Labor Market Just Plummeted - Notable Bond Market Moves

## 1. Thesis

Challenger layoffs and ADP weakness now agree the labor market is loosening from the demand side: November cuts hit 71,000 for the highest November since 2022, year-to-date cuts passed 1.10 million at the worst pace since 2020, and planned hiring fell to the lowest year-to-date level in 15 years. Claims still look calm at 216,000, but the presenter reads the divergence as gig-work masking plus a low-hire freeze meeting rising fires — and the bond market's bear-steepener with a 7-8bp wider 30s-2s spread plus a weaker dollar on Hassett-as-chair chatter is pricing fiscal dominance, not labor softness as rate relief.

## 2. Key Claims

- [00:27] November ADP private payrolls lost 32,000 jobs while the 6-month average added only 11,000 jobs per month. (as of Nov 2025 ADP print)
- [00:59] US employers announced 71,000 job cuts in November, up 24% from November last year. (as of Nov 2025 Challenger print)
- [01:16] November's 71,000 cuts mark the highest November total going back to 2022. (as of Nov 2025 Challenger print)
- [01:40] November cuts above 70,000 occurred only twice since the GFC, in 2022 and in 2008. (as of Nov 2025 Challenger history as cited)
- [01:51] Year-to-date announced cuts reached 1.10 million jobs, up 54% from the first 11 months of last year. (as of Nov 2025 Challenger print)
- [02:03] Year-to-date cuts stand at the highest level through November since 2020, when 2.20 million cuts were announced. (as of Nov 2025 Challenger history)
- [03:44] Year-to-date planned hires total only 500,000, down 35% from 761,000 at this point in 2024. (as of Nov 2025 Challenger print)
- [04:00] Planned hiring is the lowest year-to-date total in 15 years, since 392,000 hires in 2010. (as of Nov 2025 Challenger history)
- [04:40] Initial claims fell to 216,000 versus 220,000 expected, the lowest since October 2022. (as of Dec 2025 DOL print)
- [04:52] Continuing claims printed 1.94 million versus 1.96 million expected, also below market expectations. (as of Dec 2025 DOL print)

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] Challenger and ADP now tell the same demand story because layoffs are surging while hiring plans sit at a 15-year low, breaking the low-hire/low-fire freeze.
- [TAKE] Calm claims do not refute the crack because gig work absorbs displaced workers who never file, so 216,000 understates true separation pressure.
- [TAKE] The long end is pricing fiscal dominance rather than growth because the 30-year rises fastest on deficit and inflation risk while the 2-year tracks Fed expectations.
- [TAKE] A Hassett-led Fed would steepen the curve bearishly because markets expect rate cuts toward zero that lift inflation expectations and punish duration.
- [TAKE] Tariff and DOGE reason codes show policy-driven cuts layering onto AI restructuring, so the 2025 surge is not a single-cause event.
