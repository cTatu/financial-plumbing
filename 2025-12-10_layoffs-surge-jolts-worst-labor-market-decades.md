# Layoffs SURGE - JOLTS Report: Latest Read on Labor Market - The Worst Jobs Market in Decades?

## 1. Thesis

Headline JOLTS looks okay with 7.7M openings above 7.6M unemployed, but the labor market is a low-hire, low-fire freeze where hiring as a share of the labor force sits near GFC lows, the fill rate scrapes all-time lows, and layoffs rise faster than hires. That pattern — openings up without hires, quits flat, churn tilting to destruction — plus AI explicitly aimed at headcount reduction and a K-shaped economy where five firms' capex holds up GDP, argues traditional aggregates increasingly mislead while employer leverage rebuilds.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] Openings above unemployment is the historical anomaly, so the brief inversion was normalization and the current bounce does not prove strength.
- [TAKE] Openings rising far faster than quits and hires signals tightness on paper but hiring failure in practice, which gives weight to fake-posting claims.
- [TAKE] The 12-month rate-of-change cup-and-handle in openings mimics post-recession exits, suggesting a 2022 recession occurred without declaration and many sectors remain in one.
- [TAKE] Hires rising faster than quits into stress repeats the pre-2008 pattern where workers sense weakness before employers adjust postings.
- [TAKE] AI has one enterprise purpose — productivity through headcount reduction — so restructuring, DOGE downstream effects, and AI layoffs will bifurcate headline GDP from bottom-80% experience.
