# Breaking: SOFR Soars as Liquidity Stress Worsens

## 1. Thesis

Quarter-end merely stamped a trend already grinding since late July: the SOFR-minus-RRP spread is on pace for its highest weekly close since July 2019, two months before the repo crisis that forced the Fed to print, because QT is destroying reserves while the TGA's $320B-to-$757B rebuild sucks dollars out and the $3.15T overnight market has no RRP cushion left. The corroboration moved from plumbing to prices months ago — gold's $2,000 breakout lines up with the spread's turn, and MOVE plus VIX both spike on the same calendar flags — which is why this resolves either in a funding break or in the same answer as 2019: fresh reserves buying collateral.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] SOFR above the Fed's bands means dollars are scarce because the free-market overnight price only exceeds administered rates when one cash holder faces many borrowers.
- [TAKE] Quarter-end window-dressing amplifies but does not cause the strain because the spread has trended tighter since late July, well past the September 15 tax date.
- [TAKE] QT plus the TGA rebuild plus an empty RRP tank is a triple drain because destroyed reserves leave no excess buffer once banks park $49B at the Fed instead of lending it.
- [TAKE] Gold's breakout at the spread's turn is the market choosing the bearer asset with no counterparty risk ahead of the 2019-style print-and-buy-collateral response.
- [TAKE] MOVE and VIX both flagging the same calendar proves funding scarcity prices into duration and equities because fewer dollars remain to buy Treasuries.
