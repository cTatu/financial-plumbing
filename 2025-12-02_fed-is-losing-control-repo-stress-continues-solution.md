# Fed is Losing Control - Repo Stress Continues, What's the "Solution"?

Day four over the ceiling turns the squeeze from event into regime: SOFR printed 4.12% with Fed funds at 3.89% while the facility drew another $9.20B, and the presenter argues month-end mechanics cannot explain four straight days starting November 25. ## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] Four straight over-ceiling days beginning November 25 cannot be month-end window-dressing because balance-sheet cosmetics last 1.00-2.00 days and revert quickly.
- [TAKE] The expected TGA drawdown will not relieve reserves because $650.00 billion of weekly bill rolls plus $300.00 billion of coupons force a permanently large buffer.
- [TAKE] QT ending into mechanical reserve-management buying is too small because SOFR staying 37bp over the floor says the marginal dollar is still missing.
- [TAKE] Scarcity means lendable dollars, not all dollars, because reserves can exist while banks hoarding for SLR and counterparty reasons refuse to lend them.
- [TAKE] The durable fix is printed reserves for securities because only new base money injected against bank collateral rebuilds the tank the TGA drained.
