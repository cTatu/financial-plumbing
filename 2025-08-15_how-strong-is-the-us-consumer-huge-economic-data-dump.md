# How Strong is the US Consumer? -- Huge Economic Data Dump

## 1. Thesis

The consumer still spends only because prices are higher, while inflation-adjusted spending stalls: real PCE peaked in December 2024 and has declined for 6-7 months with growth near 2% versus 4-7% in healthy expansions, and real retail sales sit at 1.2% after contracting from early 2022 through September 2024. Households face a 33% rise in nominal grocery bills since 2019, sentiment fell to 58.6 with durable-buying down 14% on high prices, and industrial output has been flat since 2007 after peaking in the late 1990s — so tariffs alone cannot reshore the 5 million lost manufacturing jobs without addressing dollar overvaluation.
