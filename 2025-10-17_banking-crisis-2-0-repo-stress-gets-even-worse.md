# Banking Crisis 2.0? - Repo Stress gets EVEN WORSE

## 1. Thesis

One day after the SRF tap, funding stress deepened on a weekly basis while regional-bank equities posted their worst daily losses since the 2023 failures, joining a liquidity squeeze to a credit scare: weekly SOFR-minus-RRP reached its widest since July 2019 with SOFR 5bp over the discount-window ceiling and RRP-minus-SRF negative for the first time since 2020, all outside any calendar window. The presenter ties both legs to the same drain — a 7%-of-GDP pro-cyclical deficit, a $300B-to-$810B TGA rebuild with reserves near $3T, and QT with no $1.8T RRP buffer left as in 2023 — so the forward path repeats September 2019 with reserve creation, while LQD-over-HYG and KRE become the high-frequency watch for contagion from Zions and Western Alliance charge-offs into private credit.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] QT plus deficits plus the TGA rebuild plus an empty RRP drain dollar liquidity because every new Treasury must be bought with dollars while QT destroys reserves.
- [TAKE] RRP-minus-SRF going negative marks a shift from abundant to scarce dollars because the excess-dollar tank is empty at the moment the emergency backstop is used.
- [TAKE] SRF use collateralized mainly with mortgage-backed securities flags mortgage-market weakness because banks pledged MBS rather than Treasuries for emergency dollars.
- [TAKE] LQD-divided-by-HYG tracks credit stress at higher frequency than the BofA high-yield index because the ETF ratio reprices intraday while the index updates once per day.
- [TAKE] Regional-bank charge-offs and repo tightness are probably connected through funding pressure and private-credit ties, though the presenter disclaims expertise in leveraged loans and bank credit.
