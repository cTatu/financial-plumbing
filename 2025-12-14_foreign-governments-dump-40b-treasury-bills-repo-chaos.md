# Foreign Governments DUMP $40 BILLION of US Treasury Bonds - Was This the Cause of Repo Market Chaos?

## 1. Thesis

September TIC data shows foreign central banks sold $40B of T-bills into already-tight dollar funding, flooding collateral while draining lendable dollars just as SOFR spiked 26bp over RRP and reserves fell ~$400B — a plausible accelerant, not the root cause, of repo stress. The structural frame is unchanged: a ~$1.3T current-account deficit must be financed by equal capital inflows, so official selling plus QT/TGA drain leaves the $1.8T repo-financed basis trade one funding spike away from a forced unwind.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] [01:00] The trade deficit is the funding requirement because a $1.3T current account deficit must be matched by $1.3T of foreign buying of US securities.
- [TAKE] [03:54] Official T-bill sales tighten funding mechanically because the seller takes dollars and leaves collateral, removing lendable cash from a system already short dollars.
- [TAKE] [04:09] Elevated SOFR spreads signal dollar shortage against collateral glut because everyone holds bills while few hold free dollars to lend.
- [TAKE] [09:11] The $1.8T basis trade is the fragility point because it rolls every night in repo, so any sustained financing spike forces deleveraging of Treasury longs versus futures shorts.
- [TAKE] [09:58] Official selling amplified but did not originate the crunch because QT plus TGA rebuild had already drained ~$400B of reserves before September.
