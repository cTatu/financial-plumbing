# How the Government Shutdown Could Cause a Financial Crisis: TGA and Liquidity

## 1. Thesis

SOFR remains 13bp above the ceiling for a sixth straight day and 38bp above reverse-repo even past month-end, with $0.2 billion tapped yesterday afternoon and $2.8 billion this morning, because the Treasury General Account keeps climbing past its $850 billion rebuild target toward about $1.55 trillion as taxes flow in and nothing flows out during the shutdown. With the reverse-repo buffer empty, that $680 billion TGA build plus QT and heavy bill supply had to come from bank reserves, now $3.4 trillion down to $2.8 trillion and 13% of M2 at the pre-regional-failure line, so each extra shutdown week mechanically tightens Fed net liquidity, firms the dollar, and pressures risk until spending resumes or the Fed replaces reserves.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] The shutdown turns the TGA into a one-way drain because tax receipts still land in the Treasury's Fed account while outlays stop flowing back into banks.
- [TAKE] An empty reverse-repo facility forces the $680 billion TGA build onto reserves because the prior Yellen-era buffer that absorbed rebuilds no longer exists.
- [TAKE] QT plus TGA plus bill supply explain the reserve collapse better than calendar effects because SOFR stays above the ceiling after month-end passes.
- [TAKE] Fed net liquidity tracks the dollar because removing reserves while TGA rises tightens dollar funding and produces the recent DXY squeeze.
- [TAKE] Powell and Logan naming SOFR and IORB pressure confirms the strain is policy-relevant because the Fed rarely discusses funding plumbing unless scarcity is binding.

## 4C Predicts

- [PREDICTS] A longer shutdown keeps pushing TGA higher and reserves lower with SOFR wide. -> check: TGA toward higher highs with reserves under $2.80T and spreads wide confirms it; TGA falling on reopening with spreads compressing refutes persistence.
- [PREDICTS] The dollar squeeze extends while Fed net liquidity tightens. -> check: DXY higher with TGA up and reserves down confirms it; DXY fading with TGA down says the liquidity bid passed.
- [PREDICTS] The Fed ends QT and restarts reserve buying if repo pressure proves not temporary. -> check: QT-end plus bill-buying guidance confirms it; unchanged runoff with spreads normalizing says no action was needed.
