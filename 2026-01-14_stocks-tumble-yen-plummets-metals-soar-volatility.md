# Stocks Tumble, Yen Plummets, Metals Soar — Inside Today's Market Volatility

## 1. Thesis

A hotter-than-expected November PPI (3.0% headline and core versus 2.7% expected) combined with strong 3.5% nominal retail sales, Japan snap-election headlines, and Iran-strike fears produced a violent rotation rather than a uniform selloff: Mag-7 fell 2% with SPX down 1% and Nasdaq down 1.77% while equal-weight SPX and Russell 2000 held flat, and VIX lifted only to about 18. The tell is where the bid went — silver above $90 with a $12-13 Shanghai premium, gold to new highs, BTC up about 10% year-to-date after a weak 2025, and materials, energy, and staples ripping — while USD/JPY pushed to 159.4 into MoF jawboning and an underpriced BoJ hike window, the classic pre-intervention setup that would force Treasury sales.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] Hot PPI plus strong nominal retail sales prices out Fed cuts because PPI leads CPI by about two months while real consumption has been flat since 2022.
- [TAKE] Cap-weighted weakness with flat equal-weight and Russell breadth is rotation into cyclicals, not systemic liquidation, since materials, energy, and staples are ripping while tech lags.
- [TAKE] The Shanghai premium widening alongside silver's parabolic move since October 2025 marks Eastern physical demand, not Western futures speculation, as the marginal buyer.
- [TAKE] A 4-5-cent BoJ-hike price with USD/JPY at 159.4 and MoF "speculator" language is the classic pre-intervention setup where Japan sells dollar assets including Treasuries to buy yen.
- [TAKE] Nasdaq failing to make a new high since late October while industrials grind higher dates a durable broadening-out that favors the real economy over Mag-7 concentration.
