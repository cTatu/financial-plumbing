# Treasury Bond Yields Soar - How a Tax Strike Could Margin Call the US Govt & Crash the Bond Market

## 1. Thesis

The long end spiking to a four-month high near 4.86% on the 30-year cannot be pinned on shutdown risk (betting odds falling since early December) or fresh data (PMIs in line, oil down), so the presenter uses the rising "tax strike" chatter as a vehicle for fiscal arithmetic: if 20% of Americans withheld income tax, revenue drops $5.3T to $4.8T while 6% nominal spending growth takes $7.1T to $7.5T, forcing an extra ~$900B of supply onto a market already bear-steepening since September 2024 cuts. That supply plus MPC-driven consumption (+$140B atop 3% inflation) plus interest expense jumping $1.1T toward $1.4T forms a reflexive doom loop the Fed cannot hike out of because the debt stock is short-dated T-bills — the logical endpoint being that the dollar is backed by perceived tax capacity, not military power.
