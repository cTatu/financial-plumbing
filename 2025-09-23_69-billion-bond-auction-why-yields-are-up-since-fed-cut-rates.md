# $69 Billion Bond Auction - Why Yields are Up Since the Fed Cut Rates (A Warning)

## 1. Thesis

A $69 billion 2-year auction that technically stopped through masks the real signal: foreign demand has faded from early-year records back to 2020-2022 levels while every maturity from 2 years to 30 years has risen since the September 17 cut. That perverse rise is not the normal recession-cut pattern of 2008 or 2020 but a fiscal-dominance revolt — the Fed easing into 3% inflation, all-time-high equities and homes, and $1.2 trillion of interest at 23% of revenue — so the long end demands more term premium and gold confirms the debasement read.
