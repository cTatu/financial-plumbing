# Liquidity Stress in Repo Markets Returns - Top Fed Official Issues Shocking New Warning

## 1. Thesis

Repo stress is back outside any quarter-end window: SOFR printed above the Fed's upper bound with a quarter of the $3.20T market financing over it for weeks, and the Standing Repo Facility drew $24.60B in two days after years of disuse. With the reverse-repo buffer empty and the TGA rebuilt from $300B to $900B out of reserves, Governor Cook's financial-stability warning about the $1.80T basis trade lands as confirmation rather than commentary — the plumbing has crossed from ample to scarce liquidity.
