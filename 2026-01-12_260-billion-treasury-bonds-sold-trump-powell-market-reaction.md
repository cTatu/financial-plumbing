# $260 Billion in Treasury Bonds Sold — Right After Trump Goes Nuclear on Powell — Market Reaction

## 1. Thesis

A DOJ criminal investigation into Powell landed the day before the Treasury sold roughly $260B across four tranches ($86B 3-month, $77B 6-month, $58B 3-year, $39B 10-year), framing the session as an emerging-market fiscal-dominance test. The market answer was the opposite of a boycott: both coupon auctions stopped through with 56.5% and 70.0% indirect takedown while the Fed added $33B of reserves in one day under its ~$50B-per-month reserve-management bill buying. Price action was denominator trade — S&P 500 reversed a 0.6-0.77% overnight gap to a new all-time high in dollars, gold rose 2.5% and silver 7% to all-time highs, and the dollar softened — while the 10-year spread over Fed funds sat at a 3-year high, showing the long end still refuses to follow Fed cuts.
