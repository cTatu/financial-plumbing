# Tariffs Crash the Stock Market, Sending Nasdaq Into a Bear Market - Weekly Cross-Asset Recap

## 1. Thesis

Liberation-Day tariffs triggered a historic cross-asset selloff that pushed the Nasdaq into a bear market, but the market's own pricing rejects the tariff-inflation story: yields fell hard across the curve, global breakevens priced disinflation, and oil sliced through support as a growth scare took hold. The dollar, still grossly overvalued near 102 on purchasing-power and balance-of-payments grounds, has to weaken much further if reindustrialization is serious — and tariff policy's success will show up in the currency first. Beneath the tape, the pain is narrowly held (the top 10% own 93% of equities) while labor's share keeps losing to capital, so the correction reads as a long-overdue rebalancing toward labor rather than a buyable dip.
