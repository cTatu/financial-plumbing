# The Price of a Dollar: How the Fed Sets the Price for US Dollars

## 1. Thesis

The overnight dollar has a price, and the Fed manages it as a corridor rather than a single rate: the reverse-repo rate sets a 4.25% floor, the discount window sets a 4.50% ceiling, and interest on reserves at 4.40% sits above the 4.33% fed funds rate to keep $3.2T parked. SOFR is the free-market read on dollar supply versus demand inside that corridor — spiking far above funds when QT destroyed reserves into the September 2019 9% seizure, then sinking below the floor for over a year when QE flooded the system in 2020-2022. With SOFR again pushing above funds and IORB while reserves move sideways, the setup rhymes with 2019 tightness rather than 2021 ease, which is why QT is ending and the next turn is toward expansion.
