# The Debt End Game - What History Tells Us

## 1. Thesis

Today's 122% debt-to-GDP with negative real policy and rising longs replays the 1942-1951 playbook where war debt hit 115-118%, the Fed capped bills at 37bp and long bonds at 250bp, and inflation at 13-20% ground debt down through negative real yields rather than default. The tell is the same sequencing now: funds-cut demands toward 35-50bp plus all-bill issuance plus a captured balance sheet first steepen the curve, then force explicit caps on longs where mortgages and cards actually price — a financial-repression regime that lifts nominal assets, punishes real bondholders, and shows up first in gold up 100% since 2023.

- [01:10] Year-over-year inflation hit 13% in 1942 and reached 20% in 1947. (as of 1942/1947 prints)
- [05:43] Short rates were capped at 37 basis points and long rates at 250 basis points under yield-curve control. (as of 1942-1951 regime)
- [07:16] Federal debt expanded 8-fold from 1940 to 1945 to fund the war. (as of 1940-1945 episode)
- [09:15] Real 10-year returns troughed at negative 16.80% per year during the 1947 inflation spike. (as of 1947 episode)
- [09:41] Real rates reached a second trough near negative 6% in the early 1950s before normalization. (as of early-1950s episode)

- [15:20] Nominal growth was 6.60% with the 30-year at 5% in Oct 2023 but only 4.40% with the 30-year at 5% today. (as of Oct 2023 vs Jul 2025)
- [21:50] Short-term rates are being pushed toward 35 to 50 basis points, mirroring the 37-basis-point wartime bill cap. (as of Jul 2025 proposal)
