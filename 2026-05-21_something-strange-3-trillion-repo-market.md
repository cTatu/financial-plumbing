# Something Strange is Happening in the $3 TRILLION Repo Market

## 1. Thesis

The $3 trillion secured repo market has flipped regimes in months: from a Q3-Q4 2025 dollar shortage with SOFR above the Fed's upper bound to a May 2026 collateral shortage with SOFR collapsing toward the lower bound. The Fed's roughly $200 billion of reserve-management T-bill buying helped flip the imbalance, but drained RRP balances, $5.3 trillion in money-fund cash that cannot access Fed funds, and revived leveraged basis-trade demand for collateral now leave too much cash chasing too few T-bills — the mirror image of the 2019 shortage that preceded money printing.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] The Fed controls no single rate but a corridor that bounds the price of an overnight dollar, with SOFR as the market price that reveals cash-versus-collateral imbalance.
- [TAKE] Late-2025 stress was too few dollars chasing too much collateral, so the Fed printed bank reserves to buy T-bills and added cash while removing collateral.
- [TAKE] Current stress is the opposite mismatch of too much cash chasing too little collateral, which pushes SOFR down toward the RRP floor and the SOFR-Fed-funds spread deeply negative.
- [TAKE] The regime flip was accelerated by RRP drainage into the system plus Fed money printing, leaving non-bank cash stranded in secured markets it alone can access.
- [TAKE] A growing basis trade would explain surging collateral demand because rolling short-futures plus long-cash Treasuries at 50-100x leverage requires continuous repo financing.

## 4C Predicts

- [PREDICTS] RRP balance ticks back up while SOFR stays pinned near the lower bound if collateral scarcity persists. -> check: next NY Fed RRP and SOFR prints; renewed SOFR spike above upper bound invalidates.
- [PREDICTS] Basis-trade growth absorbs collateral and keeps SOFR-Fed-funds deeply negative. -> check: Fed/Cayman holdings revisions and dealer financing data; narrowing spread toward zero refutes.
- None stated on TGA as driver; presenter rules it out as too small to explain the SOFR-RRP collapse.
