# Trump's Shocking Plan to Crash the Dollar and 'Fix' the Debt Crisis

## 1. Thesis

Trump's impromptu press conference outlined an emerging-market funding playbook: cut the funds rate by 250bp, roll the government at 6-8 month bills until Powell exits, then term out long and lock lower 10s and 30s. That saves $185B per 50bp against a 3.30% average coupon and $1.1T interest bill, but bills are cash-like leverable collateral so the shift is stimulatory and inflationary. Long yields already bottomed before the September 2024 cuts and steepened after, so the bond market would likely push 30s-2s from ~100bp toward 250-315bp, sink the dollar from an 18-year-top toward the 70s or 60s, break housing priced off longs, and force eventual Twist/QE to cap the long end.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] Bills are cash-like with low volatility and high leverage as collateral, so shifting supply short suppresses long yields while stimulating nominal growth and inflation.
- [TAKE] Funding at 1-month to 8-month tenors makes the government beholden to the overnight rate and forces constant refinancing of maturing holders with new-debt cash.
- [TAKE] Short yields follow the funds rate but 10s and 30s price growth, inflation, and credit risk, so cuts bottomed longs and then bear-steepened the curve.
- [TAKE] The market reads sustained short-only funding as desperation from an overindebted issuer and demands higher term premium rather than lower long yields.
- [TAKE] Reindustrialization requires a much weaker dollar from overvalued levels, so the plan succeeds only if DXY breaks its 18-year uptrend toward the 70s.
