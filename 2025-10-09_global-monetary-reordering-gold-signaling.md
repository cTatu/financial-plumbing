# A Global Monetary Reordering Is Here: What Gold's Gains Are Signaling

## 1. Thesis

America's roughly $1 trillion annual current-account deficit mirrored by China's roughly $900 billion surplus no longer recycles into Treasuries and a strong dollar, because surplus dollars diverted into neutral gold lift the dollar gold price while leaving yuan gold flat, which arithmetically devalues the dollar against the yuan without a Plaza-style accord. Foreign-held Treasuries falling from 35% of US debt since QE3, the dollar share of reserves sliding for a decade, and gold overtaking Treasuries in reserve share show the shift is already underway. The read is that both economies overextended opposite models — US hyper-financialization against Chinese subsidized overcapacity — and gold strength is the market pricing a slow, multi-year meeting in the middle toward higher dollar gold and a stronger yuan.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] [02:30] The gold standard self-corrected because surplus gold inflows expanded money and forced rate hikes that strengthened the deficit country's adjustment.
- [TAKE] [07:05] The dollar's reserve status creates inelastic demand that keeps it overvalued versus twin-deficit fundamentals while China manages the yuan undervalued for share.
- [TAKE] [09:15] Surplus dollars once recycled into Treasuries kept rates low, bond prices up, and the dollar strong by construction.
- [TAKE] [10:20] Selling $400 billion of unwanted dollars for gold lifts dollar gold while yuan gold stays flat because no bilateral yuan surplus exists to sell in reverse.
- [TAKE] [17:38] The People's Bank of China cannot fully offset the shift because $9 trillion-per-day currency forces overwhelm managed-currency intervention over time.
