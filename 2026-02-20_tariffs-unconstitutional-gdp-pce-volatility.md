# Breaking: Economic Data Shocks, Major Volatility as Supreme Court Rules Tariffs Unconstitutional

## 1. Thesis

Q4 GDP printed just 1.4% versus 2.8% expected while core PCE ran 0.4% for 3.0% with wages up only 0.3%, a stagflationary miss amplified by a 1-point shutdown drag and fading AI-data-center support. Into that fragility the Supreme Court voided tariff authority, threatening $133B of refunds and ~$300B per year of revenue that had softened a 6% pro-cyclical deficit; the market read is textbook fiscal dominance — SPX whipsawed ~1% in minutes, the curve steepened 3bp as supply fear won, June-cut odds fell under incoming Chair Warsh, and gold broke out whether tariffs stay or go.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] Missing GDP while PCE runs 0.4% is fiscal-dominance proof because the Fed cuts into 58 months of above-target inflation to fund the deficit.
- [TAKE] Tariffs are a tax that lowers growth and raises measured prices unevenly, but services not goods drive inflation so repeal lifts growth while threatening the deficit.
- [TAKE] Refunding $133B from a $900B TGA is fundable, yet losing $300B per year of tariff revenue must mean more bond supply because spending will not be cut.
- [TAKE] Twin deficits mean only debt accumulates in aggregate, which is why savings fell to 3.6% with savings-to-M2 at record lows since balanced-trade 1970s.
- [TAKE] The dollar fell because tariffs compress the current-account deficit and tighten exported-dollar supply, so removing them is dollar-negative at the margin.
