# Is Trump Going to Fire Jerome Powell? - How it's Part of a Much Bigger Story

## 1. Thesis

Powell-versus-Trump is not a personality feud but a weak-dollar transmission problem: the video argues tariffs alone cannot rebalance trade while the dollar stays 40% to 80% overvalued on balance-of-payments grounds, so Trump wants lower rates either by firing Powell, appointing a shadow chair to jawbone the 2-year down, or forcing financial repression. The deeper structure is twin-deficit arithmetic — every $100-billion-plus of monthly deficit must return as foreign buying of stocks, Treasuries, and homes — which enriched the top 1% and multinationals while real wages priced in gold fell 82% and housing affordability collapsed. With debt over 120% of GDP and interest above 20% of receipts, the only historical exit is negative real rates that devalue bonds and the dollar together, which is why Turkey- and Argentina-style nominal charts warn that equity gains ahead may be pure currency debasement.
