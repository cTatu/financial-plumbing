# Inflation is Headed Higher

## 1. Thesis

Inflation bottomed around 2.2-2.3% and heads higher because the stickiest driver — services — is re-primed while the goods-side relief fades. Wage growth runs 4.7% against 2.7% headline CPI with workers still digging out of the post-2020 purchasing-power hole, and a shrinking labor pool adds upside pressure; the de-anchoring tell is Michigan one-year expectations spiking from 2.5-2.6% to 6.6% after the election. Underneath sits the monetary base case: M2 at a $22 trillion high after accelerating since November 2023, Fed net liquidity turning with TGA drawdown and QT ending, gold doubling from $1,600 to $3,300 since September 2022, and a 6.8% deficit funded in lever-able bills. Tariffs are dismissed as a 20-30-basis-point one-time tax, while the long end — 30-year pressing near 5% with the 30-minus-10 spread widening from 20 to 55 basis points — prices the fiscal path instead. Even 6-7% CPI without hyperinflation would be brutal for purchasing power, so scarcity over bonds is the prescription.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] [03:45] Services inflation is the human side of inflation, so 4.7% wage growth with workers clawing back to 2019-2020 purchasing power keeps the bias upward.
- [TAKE] [06:32] Shrinking the labor pool through restrictive immigration mechanically lifts wages because labor obeys supply and demand like any priced input.
- [TAKE] [31:04] Tariffs are a one-time consumption tax worth 20-30 basis points of CPI, not monetary inflation, and mostly invite demand destruction instead.
- [TAKE] [25:29] Inflation is the money supply expanding faster than goods and services, so a $22 trillion M2 with TGA drawdown and QT ending is the forecast base.
- [TAKE] [13:20] Bills are cash-like collateral that lever into dollars while the long end charges for fiscal and inflation risk, so all-bill funding stimulates even as 30-year yields climb.

- Watch the 30-year near 5% with the 30-minus-10 spread: spread widening past 55bp with 30-year higher confirms fiscal pricing, while spread compressing with 30-year below 4.50% says duration re-anchored.
