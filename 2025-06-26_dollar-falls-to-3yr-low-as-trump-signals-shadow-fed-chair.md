# Dollar Falls to 3yr Low as Trump Signals Shadow Fed Chair

## 1. Thesis

DXY breaking below 98 to its lowest since February 2022 is being priced as forward guidance, not just spot news: naming a dovish shadow chair 11 months early would push the 2-year down today because markets discount the promised 200-to-250-basis-point funds cut, but cutting only the overnight rate while funding the deficit at the short end steepens the curve into a bear steepener — short yields fall while 10-year and 30-year yields rise on inflation and financial-repression fears — which leaves mortgages, cars and cards tight, pressures the $30 trillion foreign carry with $10 trillion unhedged equity exposure already nursing currency losses, and ends in either a debt margin-call recession or QE that validates the long-end selloff.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] [04:36] A shadow chair works through forward guidance because the 2-year prices where funds will be in 6-to-12 months, so dovish promises lower it today.
- [TAKE] [07:09] Short-only funding plus a 200-to-250-basis-point funds cut is payday financing that markets read as inflationary financial repression.
- [TAKE] [10:45] The post-September 2024 move is a bear steepener where long yields rise faster than shorts, unlike the 2008 bull steepener where all yields fell and shorts fell fastest.
- [TAKE] [13:02] Funds cuts do not ease mortgages, auto loans or cards because those rates price off long-term yields that are rising with the steepener.
- [TAKE] [26:49] Foreigners hold both the asset and a long-dollar position, so selling the S and P 500, bonds and real estate and then selling dollars mechanically pushes DXY down 10.00%.
