# Shocking Budget Report, Tariffs Heat Up and Fed Chair to Resign?

## 1. Thesis

June's $27 billion surplus does not fix a 6%-of-GDP deficit running at 4% unemployment, because receipts at 15-17% of GDP cannot cover spending that doubled since 1950 with Social Security growing 3x GDP and interest compounding on $36 trillion. Tariff revenue at $26 billion in June explains the whole surplus and annualizes toward $300 billion, yet individuals paid $2 trillion versus $366 billion from corporations while profits outran wages 5,878% to 821% since 1971. With 35% Canada, 30% EU/Mexico, and 50% copper threats plus CBOT shorts steepening into duration, the fiscal path still points to bill funding plus rate cuts rather than balance.
