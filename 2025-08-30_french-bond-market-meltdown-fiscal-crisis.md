# French Bond Market Meltdown = Fiscal Crisis?

## 1. Thesis

France has crossed from chronic pro-cyclical deficits into an acute fiscal doom loop: a 5.8% deficit with 57% of GDP in government spending and only 1.8% nominal growth leaves the 10-year rate 1.8 points above growth, so interest at 75B euros a year forces more issuance that pushes yields higher and the deficit wider. The bond market prices it as a PIGS-style crisis with the 30-year matching Greece and the France-over-Italy spread the widest since before 2005, and with no political path to austerity plus ECB rules against direct monetization, France's $375B in Treasury holdings become the saleable buffer that transmits Paris stress into US yields.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] France runs a pro-cyclical deficit because the shortfall widened to 5.8% of GDP even as unemployment fell, so stimulus never retrenched in good times.
- [TAKE] Energy import dependence makes oil and the euro current-account levers because costlier crude and a stronger euro both widen the deficit France cannot print past.
- [TAKE] Yield minus nominal growth is the solvency gauge, and with the 10-year 1.8 points above 1.8% growth the debt ratio must rise without austerity or faster growth.
- [TAKE] The long end prices fiscal risk rather than growth because anemic 0.8% real growth with a 30-basis-point monthly jump in the 30-year is the debt-crisis signature.
- [TAKE] The doom loop runs through issuance because wider deficits mean more bond supply, which lowers bond values, raises yields and interest, and widens the deficit again.
