# Meltdown in Repo Markets- Fed Loses Control as SOFR Spreads Soar, Emergency Fed Liquidity Surges

## 1. Thesis

Friday's $50 billion all-day Standing Repo draw plus SOFR at 4.22%, 22bp above the ceiling for a fifth straight day, marks the closest brush with the March-2020 basis-trade unwind since that episode, with Monday adding another $14.7 billion morning tap outside any month- or quarter-end window. With reserves down $3.4 trillion to $2.8 trillion, the reverse-repo shock absorber empty, and heavy $110 billion-scale bill supply draining cash, the presenter reads vertical 2-year/10-year/30-year yields, a dollar stall at 100, and futures outperforming TLT cash as early unwind transmission because the $2 trillion levered cash-futures basis trade is funded overnight in the $3 trillion SOFR market where a 10bp edge at 50-100x leverage cannot survive surging carry cost.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] SOFR 22bp over the ceiling for five straight non-calendar days proves persistent stress because both the price dislocation and repeated SRF taps agree outside month- and quarter-end.
- [TAKE] An empty reverse-repo facility removes the shock absorber, so bill supply and TGA rebuilding must drain bank reserves directly and lift SOFR.
- [TAKE] The Cayman $1.8 trillion footprint identifies the basis trade as the fragile holder because a tiny tax haven cannot own that duration unlevered.
- [TAKE] Futures outperforming TLT cash with yields vertical fits an unwind footprint because closing means selling cash bonds while buying back futures.
- [TAKE] Hawkish Powell repricing plus liquidity strain jointly explain the move because higher expected policy paths and higher funding costs both push long yields up.
