# BREAKING: Foreigners Dump $61 BILLION of Treasury Bonds -- End of Reserve Currency = A Good Thing?

## 1. Thesis

October TIC shows the strong hands leaving: foreign official reserve managers sold $24.8 billion of Treasuries for a third straight month, $87 billion over 12 months and $100 billion across dollar securities, while private foreign funds dumped $36 billion in October alone and their 12-month buying pace collapsed versus $674 billion last year. The $1.3 trillion current-account deficit still forces a $1.3 trillion capital inflow — about $1.4 trillion through October — but recycling is shifting from reserve Treasuries into equities, with $714 billion of foreign private equity buying. That rotation plus dollar overvaluation explains the K-shape: a mandated $1 trillion bid lifts assets held by the top 1-10% while wages trail GDP, and only a much weaker dollar — on the order of the 46% post-Plaza fall toward DXY 50 — restores fair value and rebuilds sovereignty.
