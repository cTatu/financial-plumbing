# Fed's Emergency Liquidity Backstop Tapped Again - The Standing Repo Facility (SRF) Explained

## 1. Thesis

The Standing Repo Facility is functioning as designed but its reuse proves scarcity: created after 2019 to cap overnight rates, the $500B overnight window with $1M minimums prints bank reserves against Treasury and MBS collateral, yet stigma keeps 25% of the $3.1T SOFR market borrowing above the 4.00% ceiling while fed funds pushes 1bp below its own ceiling. With reserves down to $2.8T from $4.2T, RRP effectively empty from $2.5T, and the TGA rebuilt $320B toward $850B, the 2019 playbook says only reserve printing through asset buying restores control.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] SRF borrowing creates reserves because the New York Fed credits bank reserve accounts against Treasury and MBS collateral overnight.
- [TAKE] RRP absorbs reserves while SRF creates them, so RRP-minus-SRF below zero marks the abundant-to-scarce regime flip.
- [TAKE] Stigma sustains above-ceiling SOFR because banks pay more in the market than reveal emergency borrowing hidden for two years.
- [TAKE] Median SOFR hides stress that the 75th percentile reveals because a quarter of $3.1T can breach the ceiling while the median sits at it.
- [TAKE] Rate cuts cannot fix a reserve quantity shortage because only asset buying that prints reserves refills the banking-system monetary base.
