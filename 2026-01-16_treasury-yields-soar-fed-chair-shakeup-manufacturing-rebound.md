# Treasury Yields Soar — Major Fed Chair Shake-Up & Manufacturing Rebound?

## 1. Thesis

The 10-year pushed above 4.2% to a 4-month high with the 10Y-minus-Fed-funds spread at a 3-4-year high not because of one number but because two hawkish signals stacked: December industrial production extended a 12-13-month durable bounce with business equipment up 10% year-over-year, and Trump soured on Hassett ("good on TV, talks too much") which repriced Kalshi Fed-chair odds from roughly 45/37 Warsh/Hassett to 60/17, collapsing 2026 cut expectations. The market read Warsh as the hawk: gold initially sold off then recovered flat, the dollar found a bid, and the 2-year rose 3-5bp to ~3.59-3.60% — while underneath, the rotation that began in late October kept broadening with industrials up 12-13% since early December even as tech (XLK) still has no new high.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] Warsh repricing is the marginal rate driver because he is the hawkish anti-QE critic while Hassett is priced as the dovish administrative loyalist.
- [TAKE] A 10% business-equipment year plus 2.6% utility month shows AI data-center capex is the prop holding aggregate GDP above zero, so stripping it out leaves near-recession.
- [TAKE] The 10Y-over-Fed-funds spread at multi-year wides measures Fed impotence, because mortgages, cards, autos, and small-business loans price off the 10-year the Fed does not control.
- [TAKE] ISM soft-data contraction versus hard-data IP bounce is a divergence to respect, not dismiss, because new-orders surveys lead while production confirms.
- [TAKE] Industrials beating tech since late October with staples, energy, and healthcare at highs is healthy broadening, not narrow Mag-7 fragility.
