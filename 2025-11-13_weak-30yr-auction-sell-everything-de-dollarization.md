# Weak 30yr Bond Auction Amid a 'Sell Everything' Day - Is De-Dollarization Back?

## 1. Thesis

A soft $29B 30-year auction with a second straight tail prices fiscal risk at the long end while the front end still prices cuts: 1bp tail with 2.29 cover and 14.5% dealer takedown meets joint stocks-down, bonds-down, dollar-down price action with franc, euro, and yen firm, reviving the February-to-April sell-America rotation. The read is capital flight rather than domestic risk-off, amplified by post-mid-October-cut bear steepening and emerging-market-like data credibility talk, while record indirect bidding is discounted because levered Cayman basis-trade funds may print as indirects.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] A tail taxes taxpayers because Treasury must pay above-market yield to clear $29B of duration.
- [TAKE] Waning long-end demand versus healthy 2-year and 3-year bidding shows duration penalizes inflation and fiscal risk disproportionately.
- [TAKE] Rising primary-dealer share signals weak end-investor sponsorship because dealers are buyers of last resort.
- [TAKE] Recorded indirect strength overstates foreign sponsorship if levered Cayman basis-trade funds print as indirects.
- [TAKE] Joint stocks-down, bonds-down, and dollar-down with franc, euro, and yen up reads as textbook foreign-session capital rotation rather than domestic risk-off.
