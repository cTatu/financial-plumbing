# Boomer Retirement Complex: The Real Driver of Our Debt Crisis?

## 1. Thesis

Mandatory promises to older cohorts plus interest on the debt they required now consume essentially all federal revenue, because Medicare at 6.6x GDP, Social Security at 4.2x GDP, VA benefits at 3.2x GDP since the Gulf War, and interest at 2.6x GDP have all outrun the 15%-18% revenue share that has not moved since the 1950s — so the debt spiral is driven less by discretionary choices than by health-care costs tracking true inflation, a 2033 trust-fund cliff that converts to deficit finance, and a generational split where $1.60 trillion of student debt burdens youth while homes and 401(k)s buoy older balance sheets.
