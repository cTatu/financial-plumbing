# Fiscal Apocalypse: Spending Explodes to $17 Billion PER DAY - The Solution Will be Soaring Inflation

## 1. Thesis

The deficit is no longer cyclical but structural: spending runs ~$17B per day and ~23-25% of GDP while revenue sticks near 17-18%, so entitlements plus defense plus interest alone consume all revenue and interest compounds at 11-19% per year. Because the average coupon on marketable debt (~3.3%) sits below every point on the curve, interest can only rise as $2T-per-year deficits refinance higher — and the only politically available exit is fiscal dominance (cut funds toward 1-0%, fund in bills, run negative real rates), which liquidates the debt through inflation and a weaker dollar at the expense of non-asset holders.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] [03:42] Revenue at ~17-18% of GDP is a ceiling that coincides with recession, while spending has marched from ~17% of GDP in the mid-1950s to ~25% today.
- [TAKE] [06:22] Benefits have outrun output since 1971 because GDP grew ~2,585% while total benefits grew ~6,180% and Medicare grew ~16,325%, or 6.4x GDP.
- [TAKE] [07:50] Interest must rise because the 3.3% average coupon sits below every tenor on the current curve, so $2T of annual new debt plus rolled old debt reprices higher by construction.
- [TAKE] [08:37] Fiscal dominance is the plan because cutting funds toward 1% and funding in bills that track funds lowers measured interest only by shifting the cost to inflation and dollar weakness.
- [TAKE] [10:26] Negative real rates protect asset owners because homes and stocks rise with inflation while paycheck-to-paycheck youth with no assets absorb the cost-of-living increase unhedged.
