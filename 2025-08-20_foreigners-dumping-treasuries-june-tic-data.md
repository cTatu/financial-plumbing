# Foreigners Dumping US Treasuries Again? - June TIC Data Just Released

## 1. Thesis

June TIC shows the capital-account mirror working but rotating: headline inflows of $192 billion net came almost entirely through equities ($116 billion private plus $46 billion official) while Treasury demand evaporated — private buyers took only $3.9 billion and official institutions sold $9 billion net, with short bills down $12.4 billion and dollar banking liabilities down $60 billion. The buyer mix is the warning, not the headline total: marginal demand comes from twin-deficit Britain and fast-money havens (Cayman, Luxembourg, Ireland) while China delevers, Ireland and Switzerland trim, and Japan's shrinking yield premium threatens repatriation — consistent with a decade-long fall in foreign-held share, a post-2014 slide in dollar reserve share, and a 10-12% dollar drop funding gold rather than a milkshake bid.
