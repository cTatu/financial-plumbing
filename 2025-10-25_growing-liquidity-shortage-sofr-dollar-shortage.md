# A Growing Liquidity Shortage - SOFR Spreads Signal Growing Dollar Shortage

## 1. Thesis

The 10-day SOFR-minus-fed-funds average at 10bp is an all-time high back to 2019, and every leg agrees the system has flipped from abundant to scarce dollars: a $3 trillion secured market dwarfs a $93 billion funds market while the $2.5 trillion reverse-repo tank sits drained, TGA rebuild plus large bill auctions plus ongoing QT pull cash out, and the Standing Repo Facility is tapped on successive days with spreads moving vertical outside any calendar window. The read replays September 2019 almost mechanically — GSE cash leaves funds for repo, scarcity spills from secured into unsecured, and the only durable fix is reserve creation that swaps Treasuries and MBS for printed bank reserves rather than rate cuts.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] The SOFR-minus-fed-funds average measures system-wide dollar scarcity because secured and unsecured overnight cash draw on the same reserve pool.
- [TAKE] QT plus TGA rebuild plus large auctions plus a drained RRP all tighten liquidity because new Treasuries must be bought with dollars while QT destroys reserves.
- [TAKE] Repeated Standing Repo draws confirm banks hold collateral but lack cash because they pledge Treasuries and MBS to borrow dollars directly from the Fed.
- [TAKE] Rate cuts cannot relieve repo strain because the binding constraint is reserve quantity on the balance sheet rather than the administered price of money.
- [TAKE] The 2019 template of printing reserves to buy Treasuries and MBS is the policy put because the asset swap injects new cash into banks in days.

## 4C Predicts

- [PREDICTS] A SOFR dislocation spike brings renewed reserve printing that lifts the Fed balance sheet and bank reserves. -> check: balance sheet troughs with reserves rising and QT slowed or halted; continued runoff with spreads wider refutes it.
- [PREDICTS] Liquidity-driven volatility stays short-lived and confined to funding markets rather than lasting equity or bond damage. -> check: SOFR normalizing within days with stocks and bonds stabilizing; persistent wide spreads with spreading credit stress refutes it.
