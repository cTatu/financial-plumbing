# Yen Craters, Japanese Bond Market Intervention & Another Weak Treasury Bond Auction

## 1. Thesis

The $22 billion 30-year auction tailed by about half a basis point for the third time in four auctions, earning a C- grade for soft headline demand even as a record-low 8.6% dealer takedown with 64% indirects showed end-investor absorption. The larger shock was Japan: the surprise election of a fiscal-dove prime minister preaching renewed Abenomics triggered an almost 4% three-day collapse in the yen and a melt-up in 30-year JGB yields followed by a vertical snap-back flagged as Bank of Japan yield intervention. The read is that Japan has exited deflation into positive wage and inflation dynamics where higher yields are fundamental, so capping long yields only exports pressure into yen devaluation, while the US long end remains in a bear steepener with 5-year, 10-year, and 30-year yields all above levels prevailing when the Fed started cutting.
