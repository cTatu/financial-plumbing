# Higher Gold Prices = Falling Birth Rate? -- More Empirical Evidence for the Silent Depression

## 1. Thesis

The fertility collapse is an affordability signal priced in gold: Michael Howell's cross-country work finds each $1,000 of gold takes 0.1 children off fertility at an R-squared of 0.74 because housing — the largest child cost — is bid by excessive credit and unproductive government spending, with each new debt dollar returning only 45 cents of GDP while Medicare runs 7 times and Social Security 2 times faster than GDP. The denominator explains the transmission: the average home is up 2,556% in dollars since 1963 but down 69% in gold, the hourly wage is parabolic in dollars but at an all-time low in gold, GDP is up 5,500% while the wage share of M2 and GDP keeps falling, and the S&P is up 1,600% versus 200% for wages — so immigration-boosted GDP plus benefit spending begets debasement, hyperfinancialization, populism, and fewer children.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] Low fertility is a lifestyle choice driven by child costs because housing — funded by credit growth and monetary inflation — dominates the household budget.
- [TAKE] Homes did not gain value because a 2,556% dollar rise alongside a 69% gold fall proves the dollar denominator lost value while the structure did not.
- [TAKE] Wage growth lags GDP because immigration expands the labor supply and boosts aggregate output while mechanically suppressing the average hourly wage.
- [TAKE] Trade deficits hyperfinancialize assets because $150 billion per month of inflows must buy US stocks, bonds, and homes, lifting the S&P 1,600% against 200% for wages.
- [TAKE] Populism feeds a vicious spiral because low productivity and fertility beget state spending and debt, which beget debasement, gold strength, and worse inequality.

## 4C Predicts

- [PREDICTS] Falling labor supply alone will not durably lift wages because AI and robotics cut labor demand alongside supply. -> check: wage share of M2 rising with fertility stabilizing; wages flat-to-down with AI displacement accelerating refutes the hope.
