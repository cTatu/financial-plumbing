# Repo Market Stress Worsens, Bonds Meltdown, MAJOR Volatility in Currencies - Fed Warnings Intensify

## 1. Thesis

Funding stress is no longer calendar noise but a scarce-reserves regime: SOFR prints at the 4.00% ceiling with a 25bp premium to RRP on a plain mid-November date while fed funds pushes 2bp below its own ceiling, as QT plus a $500B-plus TGA rebuild with an empty RRP drains the $3.2T secured and $80B unsecured overnight markets. The Fed-insider timeline from Powell to Logan to Perli plus a $1.8T 50-to-100x levered Cayman basis trade funding 40% of Treasury buying frames the long-end meltdown and violent franc, euro, yen, and gilt swings as collateral-versus-cash stress spilling into duration and FX rather than isolated auction indigestion.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] Mid-November spreads matching quarter-end prove scarcity rather than calendar because window-dressing cannot explain stress on November 13.
- [TAKE] Powell breaking silence on SOFR spreads is itself the warning because chairs normally avoid wonky plumbing topics.
- [TAKE] A $1.8 trillion 100-to-1 basis trade turns repo funding into solvency risk because a small carry-leg move forces liquidation of cash Treasuries.
- [TAKE] RRP-minus-SRF going negative defines the scarce regime because more is drawn from the emergency backstop than remains in the excess-cash buffer.
- [TAKE] Violent yen, franc, euro, and gilt moves with a US bear steepener show funding stress transmitting from overnight cash into duration and carry FX.
