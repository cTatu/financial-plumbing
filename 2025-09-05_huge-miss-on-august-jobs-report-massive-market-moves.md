# Huge Miss on August Jobs Report - Massive Market Moves

## 1. Thesis

August payrolls printed just 22,000 jobs against 75,000 expected while June was cut to negative 13,000, confirming a two-and-a-half-year slide in hiring that revisions keep making worse. The softness is broad rather than headline-driven: participation sits at 62.2% versus a 67.3% peak, U6 runs at 7.9% against 4.3% U3, and 11 million-plus workers are either part-time for economic reasons or want a job but are not counted. Yet ISM services new orders at 51.4 and ISM prices feeding CPI with a 3-4 month lag point to re-accelerating nominal growth, so bonds bidding a September cut are pricing labor weakness while inflation signals point the other way.

## 4B TAKE

- [TAKE] [06:55] Revisions matter more than headlines because cutting May and June by a combined 258,000-equivalent leaves two of three months bordering negative prints.
- [TAKE] [08:48] Openings trailing unemployment is normalization rather than proof of breakage because that negative spread was the norm from 2000 through 2018.
- [TAKE] [10:50] Headline health fades once 4.7 million involuntary part-time plus 6.4 million sidelined want-a-job workers are added back to labor-market slack.
- [TAKE] [12:19] Sector mix weakens the headline because healthcare and social assistance carry gains while federal, wholesale, and manufacturing all shrink.
- [TAKE] [14:01] Nominal wage growth flatters workers because 3.7% earnings against faster broad-money expansion still leaves purchasing power falling in currency terms.

## 7. Follow-ups

- Watch next payrolls with revisions near 22K: another sub-100K print with June-August-style negative revisions confirms the 2023 downtrend, while 200K-plus with upward revisions says August was noise.
- Watch ISM services new orders above 50 with prices paid: orders holding expansion with CPI rising in 3-4 months confirms nominal re-acceleration, while orders under 48 with CPI falling says disinflation resumed.
- Watch U6 near 7.9% with participation near 62.2% and hours near 34.3: U6 rising with hours falling confirms slack widening, while U6 falling with hours rising says tightness returned.
